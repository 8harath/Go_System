Prepare Form 8892 extension for gift tax return

Use Form 8892 to request an automatic 6-month extension of time to file Form 709, when the taxpayer's not filing Form 4868 with individual income tax return or to make a payment of gift tax.
To prepare Form 8892 extension:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Extensions
    .
  2. On the
    8892 Extension Information
     tab, select the applicable print option.
  3. Indicate if the taxpayer's filed Form 4868 and is required to file Form 8892-V (payment voucher).
  4. Indicate if you wish to have Part III information printed when not required.
  5. Indicate if you wish to de-select the
    If you are applying for an automatic 6-month extension... in Part II
    .
  6. Indicate if you wish to suppress the carry of the extension payment for Form 709, Page 1.
  7. And lastly, if necessary, enter override amounts for gift tax paid and GST tax paid.
