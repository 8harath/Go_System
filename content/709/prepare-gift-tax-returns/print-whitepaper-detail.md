# Print whitepaper detail for Form 709, Schedule A, Parts 1 and 2

The whitepaper detail for Form 709, Schedule A, Parts 1 and 2 prints according to the [Schedule A, Part 1 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/i709.pdf).

The order of the whitepaper detail will be:

* Donee's name
* Relationship and donee's SSN
* Donee's address
* Short description of gift
* Long description of gift (optional entry in **Organizer**, **List of Gifts**).
