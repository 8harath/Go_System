NCD400-2610 e-file error

North Carolina Form D-400: If taxpayer and spouse (if MFJ) are full-year residents, then Form D-400, line 12 must equal line 14. NCD400-2610.
This happens when part year nonresident information is missing.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    North Carolina
    .
  2. Select
    General Information
     and go to the
    Part-year/Nonresident Information
     section.
  3. Make a selection other than
    Not applicable
    .
