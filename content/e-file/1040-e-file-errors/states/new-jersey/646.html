646 e-file error

NJ-1040NR Total Tax, Penalty contains Invalid +/or Missing Req. Data
This happens when the total tax and penalty contains invalid or missing data.

Solution

  1. In Organizer, select
    States
    , then
    New Jersey
    .
  2. Select
    Penalties and Interest
    .
  3. Go to the
    Compute Other Penalties and Interest
     section.
  4. Clear any marked checkboxes.
    note
    These can't be marked when e-filing.
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
