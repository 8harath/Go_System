F540NR-960 e-file error

If Form 540NR Line 91 [SharedResponsibilityPenalty] is present, then Form 353 must be attached.

Solution

To resolve:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    California
    ,
    Health Care
    , and then
    Shared Responsibility Penalty
    .
  2. In the
    Full Year Coverage Declaration
     section:
    1. Select yes if there's full year coverage.
      important
      If yes was already selected, select N/A and then re-select Yes. Next move on to step 3.
    2. Select no, if applicable, and the system will determine if a shared responsibility penalty is to calculate.
  3. Perform a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
