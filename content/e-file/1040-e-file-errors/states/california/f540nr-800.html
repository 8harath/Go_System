F540NR-800 e-file error

* F540NR-800: If Form 540NR, Line 123 [EstimatedTaxPenalty] amount is significant and indicates Form 5805, then Form 5805 must be attached.

Solution

To resolve:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    California
    ,
    Penalties and Interest
    .
  2. Select
    Penalties and Interest
    .
  3. In the
    Underpayment of Estimated Tax
     section, select
    Suppress penalty computation and do not print form
    .
  4. Perform a full recompute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
