# OH-IT1040-102 e-file error

```
OH-IT1040-102: The Federal Adjusted Gross Income on the unreimbursed health care expenses worksheet must equal the Federal Adjusted Gross Income reported on the Ohio IT 1040. Please correct and retransmit.
```

This happens when an amount for an unreimbursed health care expense is missing.

## Solution

- In Organizer, select **States**, **Ohio**, then **Adjustments to Federal Amounts**.
- Go to **Input for unreimbursed healthcare expenses worksheet** and enter **None**in lines that are blank because you don’t have data to enter.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
