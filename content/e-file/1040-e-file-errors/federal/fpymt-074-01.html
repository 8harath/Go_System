FPYMT-074-01 e-file error

Error

If the return is received after the due date of the return, then the RequestedPaymentDt in the IRS Payment Record must not be later than the date the return was received.

Cause

The
Requested Payment Date
 on the return can't be later than the date you transmit the return.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then select
    General Information
    .
  2. Select
    Basic Return information
    , then
    Bank Information
    .
  3. In the Direct Debit section, enter the date you'll submit the return in
    Date to be debited
    .
  4. Do a
    Full Recompute
    , re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
