F8936-035 e-file error

Form 8936, PrsnlUseNewCleanVehicleCrAmt must be equal to the sum of all Schedules A (Form 8936), PrsnlUseNewCleanVehicleCrAmt
This reject happens when sum of all Schedules A (Form 8936) Clean Vehicle Credit amount is not matching.

Solution

Follow these steps to resolve this reject.
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Credits
    , then
    Clean Vehicle Credits – 8936
    .
  2. Within
    Clean Vehicle Detail
    , select
    activity
    .
  3. Answer
    Yes
     to Question 5.
  4. Go to
    Part II and II – Credit Amount for Business/Investment use…
    .
  5. Select
    Yes
     for the question
    Did you acquire the vehicle for use or to lease to others, and not for resale?
     Answer
    No
     if you are leasing the vehicle from another person.
note
  • If this question is answered as no, the credit won’t generate on Line 9, and subsequently, the amount on Part III, line 12 will be blank.
  • For Parts II and III, answer only Question 5 and then the items under Part II. Leave 6 and 7 blank.
  • For Part IV, answer only question 6 and then the items under Part IV. Leave 5 and 7 blank.
  • For Part V, answer Questions 6 and 7 and then items under Part V. Leave 5 blank.
