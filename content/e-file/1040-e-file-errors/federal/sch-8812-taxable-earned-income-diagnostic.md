# E-file diagnostic: Schedule 8812 unreimbursed partnership expense

Fix the diagnostic error for unreimbursed nonfarm partnership expenses on the Schedule 8812 Taxable Earned Income Worksheet.

## Diagnostic message

```
Schedule 8812: Taxable Earned Income Worksheet - Line 2b must be reduced by any unreimbursed nonfarm partnership expenses deducted on Schedule E.
```

## Solution

To clear this diagnostic:

- Go to 

  **Organizer****Credits****Child Tax Credit****Child Tax Credit**

  .
- In the **Child Tax Credit - Taxable Earned Income Worksheet** section, enter the expense amount in **Unreimbursed nonfarm partnership expenses you deducted on Schedule E**.
- Run a full recompute to clear the diagnostic.
