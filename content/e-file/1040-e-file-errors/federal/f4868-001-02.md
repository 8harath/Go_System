# F4868-001-02 e-file error

## Error

```
Form 4868 can only be filed after the 'TaxPeriodEndDate' in the IRS Submission Manifest and on or before the due date to which the extension applies, unless Line 8 checkbox or Line 9 checkbox is checked.
```

## Cause

The extension can't be filed after the original due date unless line 8 or 9 is checked on Form 4868.

## Solution

If line 8 or 9 on Form 4868 applies and aren't checked, do the following:

- Go to **Organizer**, then **Payments and Extensions**.
- Open **Extensions**.
- For line 8, mark the **Taxpayer is out of the country and is a U.S. Citizen or resident** checkbox.
- For line 9, mark the **Taxpayer filed Form 1040NR and didn't receive wages subject to US income tax withholding** checkbox.
- Do a full recompute, recreate, and retransmit the return.
