IND-040-01 e-file error

Error

If 'ThirdPartyDesigneeInd' in the return has a choice of 'Yes' indicated, then Third Party Designee Phone Number must have a value.

Cause

Third-party designee is incomplete on Form 1040 Schedule 6.

Solution

  1. Go to the
    Organizer
    , then
    General Information
    .
  2. Open
    Basic Return Information
    , then
    Paid Preparer Information
    .
  3. Correct any fields in the Third-Party Designee section.
