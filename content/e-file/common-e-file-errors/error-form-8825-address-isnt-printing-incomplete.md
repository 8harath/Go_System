# Form 8825 address displays incompletely in column A Line 2

Addresses longer than 21 characters won't display completely on Form 8825.

Your property address may appear truncated when you enter it in the **Form 8825 Rental Real Estate Activity** section. The address displays incompletely on **Page 1**, **Column A**, **Line 2** when you print the form.

## Solution 1: Abbreviate the address

- Go to the **Form 8825 Rental Real Estate Activity** section.
- Review your property address in the **Address** field.
- Shorten the address to fit within 21 characters by using standard abbreviations:


  * Street: St
  * Avenue: Ave
  * Boulevard: Blvd
  * Apartment: Apt
  * Suite: Ste
- Verify the abbreviated address displays correctly.
- Go to 

  **Compute****Full Compute**

   to do a full recompute to apply the changes.

## Solution 2: Edit the PDF after completion

tip

We recommend Solution 1 if you can reasonably abbreviate your address. Use Solution 2 when you can't shorten the address without losing important information. Edited PDFs are suitable for paper filing. Check e-filing requirements before submitting modified forms electronically.

This 21-character limitation applies specifically to the **Address** field in **Form 8825**. Other address fields may have different character limits.
