R0000-204 e-file error

R0000-204: If 'QualifiedBusinessIncomeDedAmt' in the return (1040, 1040NR, 1041) has a non-zero value, then one and only one Form 8995 or Form 8995A must be present in the return.
This happens when an amount was entered for the qualified business income deduction and there is not a Form 8995 or Form 8995A present.

Solution

  1. In the Organizer, select
    General Information
    .
  2. Select
    Return and Print Options - Irrevocable
    , then
    General Options
    .
  3. In the
    QBI Print Options
     section, turn off all print selections.
  4. Do a full recompute.
  5. Turn on the print selections.
  6. For Form 8995 Line 17, enter
    NONE
    .
  7. Do a full recompute.
  8. Recreate the e-file.
