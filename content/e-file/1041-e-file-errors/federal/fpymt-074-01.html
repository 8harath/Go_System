FPYMT-074-01 e-file error

Error

If the return is received after the due date of the return, then the RequestedPaymentDt in the IRS Payment Record must not be later than the date the return was received.

Cause

The requested payment date on the return can't be later than the date the return is transmitted.

Solution

  1. Go to the
    Organizer
    , then
    General Information
    .
  2. Select
    Bank Information
    .
  3. Update
    Date to be debited
     to the date the return will be submitted.
  4. Do a Full Recompute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
