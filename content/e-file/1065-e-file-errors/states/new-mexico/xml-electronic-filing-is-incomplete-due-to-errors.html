XML electronic filing is incomplete due to errors e-file error

XML electronic filing is incomplete due to errors. Validation is not processed.
This commonly happens when you have indicated the Forms Included in e-file are for Support Use Only.

Solution

  1. In
    Organizer
    , select the
    States
    New Mexico
    E-file
     folder.
  2. Select
    Forms Included in E-file
    , then clear the
    Support use only
     checkbox.
  3. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
