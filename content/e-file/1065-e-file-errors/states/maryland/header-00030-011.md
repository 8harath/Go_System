# HEADER-00030-011 e-file error

```
HEADER-00030-011: Tax Year Begin date-day(DD) must be the first day of begin month.
```

This happens when the beginning date isn’t the 1st day of the month and the end date isn’t the last day of the month.

## Solution

- In Organizer, select **General Information** then **Basic Return Information**.
- Select **Entity Information**.
- Go to the **Dates** section.
- Change the fiscal year dates so that the beginning is the 1st day of the month and the ending is the last day of the month.
- Run a full recompute and recreate the e-file for the state.
- Then change the dates back for the federal.

  note

  According to the state of Maryland, their e-filing system doesn’t recognize a tax year beginning other than the 1st day of the month and ending the last day of the month. They realize that may not match the federal return.
