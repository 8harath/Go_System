# E-file diagnostic: Form 510: Line 19 Total Balance Due

```
Form 510: Line 19 Total Balance Due - must be equal to the sum of lines 17 and 18.
```

This happens when the lines previous don’t add up to the total. None being on a line can cause this diagnostic.

## Solution

- Go to the **Tax Forms**,**States** folder.
- Then go to the **Maryland, MD 510** folder.
- Open the **MD 510**, **Page 2** tab.
- If lines 17 and 18 aren't populated and line 20 has a **None** value, then for line 20 override the **None** to be blank.
- Do a Full Recompute and recreate the e-file.
