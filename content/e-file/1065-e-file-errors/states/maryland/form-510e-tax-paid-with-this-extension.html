E-file diagnostic: Form 510E tax paid with this extension

Form 510E: Tax paid with this extension must be equal to payment amount of financial transaction.
You get this error when tax paid doesn't equal the payment amount.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    Maryland
    , and then
    MD 510E
    .
  2. Remove NONE from
    Tax Paid With this Extension
    .
    1. Go to
      Organizer
      , and select
      States
      .
    2. Select
      Common State
      Estimates and Extensions
      Extension Detail
       .
    3. Go to the
      Maryland
       section and remove
      NONE
      .
  3. Then disable the Maryland extension:
    1. In
      Organizer
      States
       select
      State E-file
      .
    2. Select
      Enable/Create Extensions
       and clear the
      Enable
       checkbox for Maryland.
  4. Do a full recompute, recreate the e-file, then resubmit.
