ScheduleK1-61020-010 e-file error

ScheduleK1-61020-010: Members Income Line 2 - Distributive or prorata share of income allocable to Maryland (Nonresidents/Residents) must be present.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Maryland
    ,
    E-file
    , then
    Additional Information
    .
  2. Mark the checkboxes for the following:
    1. Suppress Individual member PTE K-1 from E-file
    2. Acknowledgement of Individual Member PTE K-1 not included in E-file
  3. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, then resubmit.
