E-file error: 1065 Element name: 'Name' is incomplete or empty

Content for field 'Name' is incomplete.
Field 'Name' cannot be empty
This happens when first or last name is missing.

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
     and
    Maryland
    .
  2. Select
    MD 510
    ,
    Sch B
    ,
    Part I
    ,
    IND yellow workpaper there are some blank partner
    s.
  3. Delete the data from the blank or deleted partners or update the details.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
     and select
    Partner by Partner Data
    .
  2. Select
    Columnar Partner Entry
    ,
    Reformat Partner Name for State Purpose
    , then
    First Name and Last Name
    .
  3. Remove the override and then drill down to edit the First and Last name of the Partners.
  4. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, then resubmit.

Solution 3

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , then
    Maryland
    .
  2. Select
    E-file
    ,
    Attachments
    ,
    K-1 Aggregation
    , then
    Summary
    .
  3. Check the box for
    Suppress system generated Sch B - Part 1 Individual Member Information
    .
