E-file diagnostic: Form 510: Line 19 Total Balance Due

Form 510: Line 19 Total Balance Due - must be equal to the sum of lines 17 and 18.
This happens when the lines previous don’t add up to the total. None being on a line can cause this diagnostic.

Solution

  1. Go to the
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    folder.
  2. Then go to the
    Maryland, MD 510
    folder.
  3. Open the
    MD 510
    ,
    Page 2
     tab.
  4. If lines 17 and 18 aren't populated and line 20 has a
    None
     value, then for line 20 override the
    None
     to be blank.
  5. Do a Full Recompute and recreate the e-file.
