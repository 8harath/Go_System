FINTRAN-98150 e-file error

State Payment Amount in Financial Transaction, if present, must be greater than 0.
This happens when financial transaction information has been entered with NONE for the amount to be paid.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , then
    Common state
    .
  2. Select
    General information
    , then
    Bank information
    .
  3. On the
    Direct Debit
     tab, remove the routing number, account number, and amount to be debited (if NONE was populated).
note
If no tax is due, view the note on the Maryland form 510E: "If no tax is due with this extension, do not mail this paper form unless it is the first filing of the entity, instead file the extension at: www.marylandtaxes.gov or call 410-260-7829 from central Maryland or 1-800-260-3664 from elsewhere to telefile this form."
