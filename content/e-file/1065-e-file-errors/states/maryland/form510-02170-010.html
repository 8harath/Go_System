Form 510-02170-010 e-file error

Line 3b Maryland apportionment factor - if greater than 0.000001, it should be equal to Maryland apportionment factor on Line 4, Schedule A
This happens when the Form MD 510 Page 4 Line 14 is not equal to Form MD 510 Page 1 Line 3b.
Solution
  1. In the
    Organizer
    , go to the
    States
     folder and select
    Maryland
    .
  2. Select
    State Information
     and
    Tax Computation
    .
  3. Go to
    Allocation of Income - Form 510
    , and select
    Partnership uses separate accounting for Maryland
    .
  4. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    Maryland
    ,
    Form MD 510 Page 4
    ,
    Line 1A Column 2
    .
  5. Make an entry or populate with NONE.
  6. Run a full recompute and recreate e-file for Maryland.
