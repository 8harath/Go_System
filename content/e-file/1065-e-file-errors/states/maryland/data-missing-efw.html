E-file diagnostic: Data Missing: Electronic funds withdrawal

Data Missing: If the checkbox to authorize the State of Maryland to initiate an electronic funds withdrawal payment, then all of the following fields must be populated with data: Electronic Funds Withdrawal (direct debit) consent; Account Type; Account Holder Type; Routing Transit Number; Bank Account Number; Amount to be Debited; Date Amount Due is to be Debited; ID Number; Account Holder Name; Total Amount Due.
This happens when you are missing bank information for the state.

    Tax Forms

    States
     folder.

    Common State
    , then the
    General Information
     folder.
  3. Go to the
    Bank Information
     folder.
  4. In
    Column A
    , go to
    Maryland
    .
  5. Fill in
    Column D
    ,
    E
    ,
    F
    ,
    G
    ,
    H
    ,
    J
     and
    K
    .
  6. Run a full recompute, then recreate the e-file.
