# E-file diagnostic: Form 510, Line 13 Total nonresident tax

```
From 510: Line 13 Total nonresident tax - must equal the sum of line 9 and line 12.
```

This happens when for state Maryland Form 510 page 2 line 13 Total nonresident tax is not equal to the sum of line 9 and line 12.

## Solution

- Go to 

  **Tax forms****States****Maryland**

  .
- Select the **Form MD 510 Page 2** tab.
- Remove **NONE** by using a space bar override on **Line 9** and **Line 11**.
- Do a full recompute.
- Recreate the e-file.
