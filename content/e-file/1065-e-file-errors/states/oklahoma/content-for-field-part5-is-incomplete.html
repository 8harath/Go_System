E-file error: Content for field Part5 is incomplete

Form: FormOK514 Part5
Requirement: Expecting Partners
Error: Content for field 'Part5' is incomplete
You need to complete Form 514, Part 5.

Solution

Per Oklahoma instructions, you'll need to complete Form 514, Part 5. Since we don't separately state Oklahoma information on the federal Schedule K-1, a federal attachment isn't sufficient.
You need to either generate partner information within the return to populate Form 514, Page 4, Part 5, or use a K-1 aggregation file, with the OK 514, Part 5 partner data populated within the e-file.
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
     and select
    Oklahoma
    .
  2. Go to
    E-File
    ,
    Attachments
    ,
    K-1 Aggregation
     and select
    Summary
    .
  3. If
    Suppress partner information for 514, Part 5
     has been suppressed, you need to attach a K-1 aggregation file named
    Form514Part5.pdf
    .
  4. Otherwise unselect that checkbox and make sure the partner data is entered and populating on OK 514, Page 4, Part 5.
  5. Perform a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
