# E-file error: Field 'MSPassThroughEntityAddress' cannot be empty

```
Field 'MSPassThroughEntityAddress' cannot be empty
```

## Solution 1

- Go to **Organizer**, **States**, **Mississippi**, then **Pass-Through Information**.
- Go to the Form 84-105 Part IV Entity Officer Information section.
- Enter **Officer Name**, **Title**, **SSN**, **% Ownership**, and **Address** fields.
- Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.

## Solution 2

- Go to **Organizer**, **States**, **Mississippi**, then **Return Options**.
- Change **Apportionment Method** to another option.
- Do a full re-compute.
- Change the **Apportionment Method** option back to the correct selection.
- Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
