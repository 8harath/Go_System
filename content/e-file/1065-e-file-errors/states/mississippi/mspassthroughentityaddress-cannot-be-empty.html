E-file error: Field 'MSPassThroughEntityAddress' cannot be empty

Field 'MSPassThroughEntityAddress' cannot be empty

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Mississippi
    , then
    Pass-Through Information
    .
  2. Go to the Form 84-105 Part IV Entity Officer Information section.
  3. Enter
    Officer Name
    ,
    Title
    ,
    SSN
    ,
    % Ownership
    , and
    Address
     fields.
  4. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Mississippi
    , then
    Return Options
    .
  2. Change
    Apportionment Method
     to another option.
  3. Do a full re-compute.
  4. Change the
    Apportionment Method
     option back to the correct selection.
  5. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
