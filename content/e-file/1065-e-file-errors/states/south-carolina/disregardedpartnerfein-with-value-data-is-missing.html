E-file error: The field 'DisregardedPartnerFEIN' with value '', data is missing

The field 'DisregardedPartnerFEIN' with value '', data is missing.
You'll get this error if the disregarded partner information contains a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN).

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    ,
    Partner by Partner Data
    , then select the partner name.
  2. Select
    Partner Information
    ,
    Partner Information
    ,
    Disregarded Entity
    , then
    Taxpayer identification number (TIN)
    .
  3. Clear the number.
  4. Do a full recompute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
