E-file error: PartnerPhone

Field 'PartnerPhone' cannot be empty

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    ,
    Partner by Partner Data
    ,
    [Partner name]
    ,
    Partner Information
    , then the
    Partner Information
     tab.
  2. Go to the
    Name, Address, Phone Number and SSN \ EIN
     section.
  3. Enter or update the phone number.
  4. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    ,
    Partner by Partner Data
    ,
    [Partner name]
    ,
    Partner Information
    , then the
    State Partner Information
     tab.
  2. Go to the
    Utah
     section and enter the
    Partner affiliation date
    .
  3. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
note
If using you're using Direct K-1 Import, advise your client to update the phone number and affiliated date for missing partners. Then do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
