E-file diagnostic: 65_166 Schedule N

Error

65_166: Schedule N : Calculation Error : Total of Column I values.

Cause

This happens when you are missing the information that required by the state for e-Filing.

Solution

  1. In Tax forms, select
    States
    , then
    Utah
    .
  2. Select
    Schedule N
    .
  3. On Schedule N line 1B, select
    To Review Detail for Schedule N
    .
  4. Select
    To Review Partner Details
    .
  5. Make sure
    Line I Tax Paid by PTE
     is populated for all partners.
  6. All partners must have column I populated if there is an override entered in Organizer, in
    States
    , on the
    Utah
     window.
  7. Select
    State Adjustments
    .
  8. In the Schedule N (Override) section, on line
    Schedule N Tax Paid by PTE
     (Override) enter the amount and allocation code.
  9. On line
    Schedule N attributable income (Override)
     enter the amount and allocation code to populate column E.
  10. Do a Full recompute and recreate the e-file.
