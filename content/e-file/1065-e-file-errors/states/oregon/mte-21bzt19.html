MTE 21BZT19 Form P e-file error

MTE 21BZT19 Form P 'Not allowed if 'IncBeforeOpLossDdctMLT' is a loss.: OperatingLossDeductMLT cannot be more than 75% of IncBeforeOplossDdctMLT.
This happens when the Net operating loss is more than 75%.

Solution

  1. In Tax Forms, select
    Cities and Intangibles
    , then
    Portland, Multnomah
    .
  2. Select
    Metro Supportive Housing Tax Return
    , then
    METBIT-65
    .
  3. Review the net operating loss on line 12. Loss can't be more than operating loss and can't be more than 75% of line 11.
To make corrections to the amounts:
  1. In Organizer, select
    Cities and Intangibles
    , then
    Portland, Multnomah
    .
  2. Select
    City and County Adjustments
    .
  3. In the METBIT-65 Entry section, review if there’s any entry or make a correction in this field.
  4. Paper file the return.
