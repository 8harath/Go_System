E-file diagnostic: Either a Schedule OC1 or Schedule OC2 must be submitted

Oregon Composite E-File: Either a Schedule OC1 or Schedule OC2 must be submitted with a Form OC. At least one partner must be included on Form OC that populates for either Schedule OC1 or OC2. To add partners to the composite go to Organizer/Partner Information/Partner by Partner Data/Columnar Partner Entry/Partners State Composites - Enter "X" in OR column for partners to be included on Form OC.
This happens when you are missing at least 1 partner on Form OC that populates for either Schedule OC1 or OC2.

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Oregon
     folder.
  2. Select
    Non-Resident Information
    .
  3. Go to
    Composite Return
    ,
    Non-Resident Partners
     folder.
  4. Select
    Partner Name
    , then a
    Type of Owner
     and
    Tax Type
    .
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    ,
    Partner by Partner Data
     folder.
  2. Select
    Columnar Partner Entry
    .
  3. Go to the
    Partners State Composites
     tab.
  4. Enter
    X
     in the
    OR column
     for the partners to include on Form OC.
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
