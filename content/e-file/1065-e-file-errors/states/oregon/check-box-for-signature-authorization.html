Checkbox for signature authorization e-file error 

Check box for signature authorization needs to be complete in Organizer/ States/Oregon/E-file or E-file Composite/Additional Information
This happens when the checkbox to authorize the composite e-file isn't marked.

Solution

  1. Go to:
    Organizer
    States
    Oregon E-File – Composite
    Additional Information
    .
  2. Mark the checkbox to agree with
    ERO / Preparer certification and signature (OC)
    .
  3. Mark the checkbox to agree with
    ACH Transfer Authorization (OC)
    .
  4. Mark the checkboxes for the authorization for
    Oregon, E-file, Additional information
    .
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
