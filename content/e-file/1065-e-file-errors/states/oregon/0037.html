0037 e-file error

* 0037: Oregon partnership return was filed. Oregon Schedule OR-AP is missing. The Schedule OR-AP is required when ReturnDataState InandOutofStateActivitityInd is true
This happens when in and out of state activity is true but there is no amounts for state allocation and apportionment for Oregon.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Allocation and Apportionment
    Data entry
    .
  2. Go to the
    Sales Factor
    ,
    Rent and Royalties
     folder.
  3. Enter
    none
     for
    Oregon
     in the
    Rents
     or
    Royalties
     column.
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
