Solution

  1. In Organizer, go to the
    States
     folder.
  2. Select
    Wisconsin
    , then
    Allocation and Apportionment
    .
  3. Select the
    Allocation and Apportionment
     tab and go to the
    Computer Options
     section.
  4. Select
    A-01
     from the dropdown.
  5. Go to
    Tax Forms
     and select the
    States
     folder.
  6. Select
    Wisconsin
    ,
    Apportionment
    , then
    A-01, Page1
    .
  7. Make sure there's an amount in line
    11
    .
  8. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
