E-file diagnostic: WI Form 3 page 2 line 19 apportionment indicator is A01 

WI Form 3 page 2 line 19 apportionment indicator is A01. However Sch A01 has no apportionment data. Tax form / Wisconsin / Apportionment / A-01
This happens when you are missing information that is required by the state or updating apportionment information.

Solution

  1. In Organizer, select
    States
    , then the
    Allocation and Apportionment
    folder.
  2. Select
    Organizer/Overrides
    , then the
    Wisconsin
     folder.
  3. Select
    Payroll and Sales
    .
  4. Go to the Wisconsin Payroll Apportionment section.
  5. In line
    Within State to Within
     , enter
    NONE
    or the apportionment values in the
    Within
     and
    Everywhere
     column.
  6. Then do a full Recompute.
  7. Recreate the e-file.
