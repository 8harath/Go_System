Solution 1

Form 05-167 isn't required if the entity is a legally formed corporation, limited liability company, limited partnership, professional association, or financial institution. To indicate this on the return:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Texas
    General Information
    General Information
    .
  2. Answer
    Yes
     for
    Corporation, limited liability company, professional association, limited partnership or financial ownership
    .

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Texas
    Information Report
    Ownership Information Report
    10% or More Owner
    .
  2. Add an owner with 10% or more ownership of the entity.
