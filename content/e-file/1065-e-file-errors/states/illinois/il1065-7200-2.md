# IL1065-7200-2 e-file error

```
* IL1065-7200-2: Form IL-1065 Step 5 Line 32 must equal all occurrences of Sch K1T Step 5 Lines 36a, 36b, 37, 38, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45 Column A, plus sum of all occurrences of Sch K1P Step 5 Lines 38a, 38b, 44, 45, 46, 47 Column A. Users must enter Sch K1P Step 5 or Sch K1T Step 5 in Organizer / States / Illinois / State Adjustments / Pass Through Input.
```

This happens when the amounts on Form IL-1065 Step 5 Line 32 don’t match Sch K1T Step 5 Lines 36a, 36b, 37, 38, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45 Column A plus the sum of all Sch K1P Step 5 Lines 38a, 38b, 44, 45, 46, 47, Column A.

## Solution 1

- In **Tax Forms**, select the **States** folder.
- Select the 

  **Illinois****Sch K-1-P****(K-1 name)**

   folder.
- Go to the **Sch K-1-P, Page 2** tab.
- Take note of the amount on **Line 46 Column A**.
- Go to the **IL-1065** folder, then **IL-1065, Page 2** tab.
- Go to **Step 5**, **Line 32**.
- **Line 32** and **Line 46** mentioned earlier need to match for each K-1.

## Solution 2

- In **Organizer**, select the **States** folder.
- Go to the 

  **Illinois****State Adjustments****Pass Through Input**

   folder.
- Add a **K-1-P** or **K-1-T** and fill out the details.
- Go to **State Adjustments**, then **State Adjustment** folder.
- Remove any data under **Distributive share of additions** and **Distributive share of subtractions line**.
- Run a full recompute and recreate the e-file.

note

Grantor trusts should be included with individuals since they are disregarded and not taxed at trust rates.
