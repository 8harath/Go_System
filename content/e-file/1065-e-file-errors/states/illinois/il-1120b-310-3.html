IL1120B-310-3 e-file error

IL1120B-310-3: If Sch B Section A Line 3 is less than zero, then Sch B Section A Line 3 must equal either Form IL-1120-ST Step 4 Line 20 or Form IL-1065 Step 4 Line 21 written as a positive.
This happens when Form IL-1120 Sch B Section A Line 3 is less than 0.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    States
    Illinois
    IL-1065
    IL-1065 Page 2
    Step 4 Line 21
    . Make sure this line has a positive number.
  2. Go to
    Tax Forms
    States
    Illinois
    Schedule B-Partner Identification
    Schedule B
    Schedule B, Page 1
    Section A
    Line 3
    . Make sure this line matches the number in step 2.
  3. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
