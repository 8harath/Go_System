IL1120B-310-1 e-file error

If any occurrence of SchILB Line Section B Col A (U.S. State Abbr) or SchILB Line Section B Col A (Foreign Country) are present, then SchILB Section A Line 3 must equal the sum of all occurrences of SchILB Line Section B Col E when the same occurrence of SchILB Line Section B Col D contains an X. (IL1120B-310-1)
This happens when the amounts on the Schedule B Summary Column E don’t agree with the Schedule B, page 1, line 3 amount. All amounts need to be rounded to the nearest whole dollar on the Illinois Schedule B, Page 2.

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
     then select
    Illinois
    .
  2. Select
    Sch B-Partner Identification
     then
    Schedule B
    .
  3. Go to
    Schedule B, Page 2
    , and select
    Schedule B Summary
    .
  4. Review the amounts. Round any amounts to the nearest whole dollar, no decimal points.
  5. Additionally,
    Total amount of base income/loss distributable (E)
    in column E needs to equal the result from Form IL-1065:
    1. Step 5, Line 35 + Line 26 + Line 27 - Line 21.
  6. Do a full recompute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit the return.
