IL1065-8400-3 e-file error

IL1065-8400-3: If Form IL-1065 Step 6 Line 39 is greater than zero and Form IL-1065 Step 6 Line 40 and Form IL-1065 Step 6 Line 41 are zero, then Form IL-1065 Step 6 Line 42 must be 1.000000.
This happens when Form IL-1120 Page 2 Line 39 is greater than 0, and Lines 40 and 41 equal 0, then Line 42 needs to equal 1.000000.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    Illinois
     folder.
  2. Make sure that
    Form IL-1065 Page 2 Step 6 line 42
     doesn't say
    NONE
     unless
    Line 39
     is less than 0.
  3. If you need to enter
    NONE
     in this field, you need to file the return on paper.
note
As per the Form IL-1065 Instructions, for Line 42: Divide
Line 41
 by
Line 40
 and enter the result, rounded to 6 decimal points. The result can't be greater than 1 or less than 0.
Refer to the Illinois 1065 Instructions for more information.
