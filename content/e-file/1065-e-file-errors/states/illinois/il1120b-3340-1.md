# IL1120B-3340-1 e-file error

```
IL1120B-3340-1: The sum of all occurrences of SchILB Section B Line L must equal the sum of all occurrences of SchILK1P Step 7 Line 53a and SchILK1T Step 7 Line 50.
```

This happens when the total amount reported on Line L of Section B across all Illinois Schedule IL-B forms is not equal to the total of all Sch. K-1-P line 53A plus Sch K-1-T line 50.

## Solution 1

- Go to **Organizer**, **States**, **Illinois** folder.
- Select the **State Adjustments** folder, then the **Pass Through Input** folder.
- Open the **Schedule K-1-P** folder, then **Name** (if it doesn't exist, you'll need to create one).
- Go to **Line 53a** and populate the amount of withholding here for each entity.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.

## Solution 2

- Go to **Organizer**, **States**, **Illinois** folder.
- Select the **State Adjustments** folder, then the **Pass share** folder.
- Go to **Pass Through Entity Tax Credit Overrides**.
- Go to the **PTE Tax Credit Received Distributed to Members Amount** line.
- Enter the amount for **Column L**.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
