ILHeader-Corp-3700-2 e-file error

1065 Illinois ILHeader-Corp-3700-2: If preparer name is present and preparer is not self-employed, then preparer's signature date, PTIN, preparer firm's name and EIN, preparer's US address or foreign address, and preparer's domestic or foreign phone must be provided. Navigate to Organizer \ States \ Common States \ General Information \ Paid Preparer Information \ Preparer Information tab, complete this section.
You'll receive this message if the signature date and email are missing in the Preparer Information tab.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Common State
    .
  2. Select
    General Information
    , then
    Paid Preparer Information
    .
  3. Select the
    Preparer information
     tab.
  4. Enter the email in
    Column G
     and the signature date in
    Column H
    .
  5. Do a full recompute then recreate the e-file.
