# IL1065-5700-2 and IL1065-7200-2 e-file errors

```
reject code IL1065-5700-2,If Form IL-1065 Step 4 Line 19 is greater than zero, then Form IL-1065 Step 4 Line 19 must equal all occurrences of Sch K1T Step 5 Lines 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35 Column A, plus sum of all occurrences of Sch K1P Step 5 Lines 32, 33, 34, 35, 36, 37 Column A. reject code IL1065-7200-2,If Form IL-1065 Step 5 Line 32 is greater than zero, then Form IL-1065 Step 5 Line 32 must equal all occurrences of Sch K1T Step 5 Lines 36a, 36b, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, 46 Column A plus sum of all occurrences of Sch K1P Step 5 Lines 38a, 38b, 44, 45, 46, ,47 Column A.
```

These diagnostics generate when Form IL-1065, Step 4, Line 19 and/or Step 5, Line 32 have data, but Schedule K-1-P or Schedule K-1-T information has not been added to the return.

## Solution

- In **Organizer** go to **States** then **Illinois**.
- Select **State Adjustments** then **Pass Through Input**.
- If you haven't already added a **Schedule K-1-P** or **Schedule K-1-T** and the information to support Form IL-1065, Step 4, Line 19 and/or Step 5, Line 32, do so now.
- If you have data in **Schedule K-1-P**, make sure:


  - entries in the first column for Step 5 Subtractions have to match the amount on Form IL-1065, Step 5, line 32.
  - entries in the first column for Step 5 Additions have to match the amount on Form IL-1065, Step 4, line 19.
- Go to the **State Adjustments** screen in the **State Adjustments** folder, and remove any amount entered on the distributive share of additions/subtractions line. This should only pull from K-1-P input.

note

Grantor trusts should be included with the individuals since they are disregarded and not taxed at trust rates.
