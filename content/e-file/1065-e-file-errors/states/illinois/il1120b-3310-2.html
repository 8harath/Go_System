IL1120B-3310-2 e-file error

IL1120B-3310-2: If FormIL1065 is attached then the sum of all occurrences of SchILB Section B Line K cannot be greater than FormIL1065 Step 8 Line 61.
This happens when IL-1065 Page 3 Line 61 doesn’t match the Partner amounts on Schedule B Summary Column K.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    States
    Illinois
    .
  2. Make sure
    IL-1065, IL-1065, Page 3, Step 9, Line 61
     is equal to the total Partners.
  3. Go to
    Tax Forms
    States
    Illinois
    .
  4. Select
    Sch B Partner Identification
    , then
    Schedule B
    .
  5. Go to
    Schedule B, Page 2
     and select
    Schedule B Summary
    .
  6. Make sure Column K amounts match Line 61.
  7. Do a full recompute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit the return.
