ILK1P-4000-1 e-file error

ILK1P-4000-1: SchILK1P Step 3 Line 19 Col A must equal the sum of all occurrences of SchILK1P Step 3 Line 19 (resident amt.).
This happens when the description is missing for Sch K-1-P step 3 line 19.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Illinois
    State Adjustments
    .
  2. Select
    Pass Through Input, Schedule K-1-P
    , then
    [Name of Schedule] K-1-P
    .
  3. Go to
    Step 3 – Share of Nonbusiness Income
    .
  4. On
    Line 19 Other Nonbusiness Income
    , enter a description for any lines that have amounts in Column A or Column B.
  5. Run a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
