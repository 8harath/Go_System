IL1065-4600-2 e-file error

IL1065-4600-2: FormIL1065 Step 3 Line 11 (Total) must equal the total of all occurrence of FormIL1065 Step 3 Line 11 (Amount).
This happens when Form IL-1065, Page 1, Line 11
Other expenses
 does not equal the amount of
itemized other expenses
 entered.

Solution

  1. In Tax Forms, select
    States
    , then
    Illinois
    .
  2. Go to
    IL-1065, line 11
     and make sure this line doesn't have an override.
  3. Drill down on line 11.
  4. Make sure that the line for:
    Other deductions
    is not overridden.
  5. If you need to correct the amount, drill down on the line,
    Other expenses
    , and then enter the appropriate expenses in Organizer.
  6. Once the amounts are correct, select
    Compute
    , then
    Full Recompute
    .
  7. Then in Organizer, select
    States
    , then
    State E-File
    , then
    Enable/create Returns
    .
  8. Select
    Create
     on row 31 for Illinois.
