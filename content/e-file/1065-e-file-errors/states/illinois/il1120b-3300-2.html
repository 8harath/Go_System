IL1120B-3300-2 e-file error

IL1120B-3300-2: Sch B Section A Line 5 must equal the sum of all occurrences of Sch B Section B Line J.
This happens when Schedule A, line 5, doesn’t match Schedule B section B line J.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    States
    Illinois
    .
  2. Select
    Sch B-Partner Identification
    , then
    Schedule B
    .
  3. Go to
    Page 1, Section A
    .
    Line 5
     must equal
    Schedule B, section B, line J
    .
  4. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    Illinois
    .
  5. Go to
    Sch B-Partner Identification
     and select
    Schedule B
    .
  6. Go to
    Page 2, Section B
     and select
    Schedule B Summary
    .
  7. To enter the amount on Column J:
    1. Go to
      Organizer
      Partner Information
      Partner by Partner Data
      .
    2. Select the
      Partner
      , then the
      State Information
       tab.
    3. Go to the
      Illinois
       section, and enter the amount on the
      Pass-through Entity Payment (Override)
       line.
  8. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
