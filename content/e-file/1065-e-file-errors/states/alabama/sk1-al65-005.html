SK1-AL65-005 e-file error

SK1-AL65-005: AL Schedule K-1 Part II Line 6, if Partner is a Nonresident is checked then State of Legal Residence must be populated.
This happens when Schedule K-1 Part II Line 6, if Partner is a Nonresident is checked, but State of Legal Residence isn't populated.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    Alabama
    , then the
    Sch K-1
     folder.
  2. Select the
    Partner Name
    . If
    Line 6
     has an
    X
    , enter the
    State of Legal Residence
    .
  3. To update
    State of Legal Residence
    , go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Alabama
    , and the
    Partner Information
     folder.
  4. Select
    Partner Name
    .
  5. Enter the state name in the
    Nonresident state of legal residence (Override)
     line.
  6. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
