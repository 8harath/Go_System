SK1-AL65-007 e-file error

SK1-AL65-007: AL Schedule K-1, if DisregardedEntity is checked then Owner's ID must be entered
This happens when partner information is missing.

Solution

Verify which partners are marked disregarded entity.
  1. In
    Organizer
    , select
    Partner
    Information
    , then
    Partner by Partner Data
    .
  2. Select
    Columnar Partner Entity
    , then the
    Designations
     tab.
  3. In the
    Partners' Disregarded Entity Information
     section, check which partners have column
    Enter X if Partner is a Disregarded Entity (DE)
     marked.
  4. Either remove the X or enter the
    Partner Name
     and
    TIN
    :
    1. Select the partner’s name in the
      Partner by Partner Data
       folder.
    2. On the
      Partner Information
       tab, enter the
      Partner Name
       and
      TIN
      .
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
