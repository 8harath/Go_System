SK1-AL65-030 e-file error

SK1-AL65-030: The total of all K-1s, Line R, Column 1, Other Deductions should equal Schedule K, Line 14, Other Deductions Apportioned Amount.
This happens when the total of all K-1s, Line R, Column 1, Other Deductions amounts don't equal the Schedule K, Line 14, Other Deductions Apportioned amount.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    , and the
    Alabama
     folder.
  2. Select
    Form 65
    , go to
    Page 4
    ,
    Line 14
    . It needs to be equal to all K-1s, Line R, Column 1, Other Deductions.
  3. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    , and the
    Alabama
     folder.
  4. Go to
    Sch K-1
    , and total of all K-1s
    Line R
    ,
    Column 1
     amount.
  5. To update the amount, go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , and the
    Alabama
     folder.
  6. Select
    State Adjustments
    , and go to the
    Income and Expenses
     tab.
  7. Go to the
    Deductions
     section.
  8. In
    Line Other
    , select
    Other Deductions
    .
  9. Enter the amounts in the necessary lines.
  10. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
