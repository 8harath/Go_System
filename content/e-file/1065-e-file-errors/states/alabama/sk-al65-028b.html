SK-AL65-028b e-file error

SK-AL65-028b: Form 65, Page 4, Schedule K Line 20c, if Alabama Amount (Apportioned Amount) is populated then the amount should equal to the detail total.
This happens when the sum of all shareholder's Part 3 Line Y amount isn't equal to the Alabama Apportioned Amount.
Solution
  1. Go to the
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    , and the
    Alabama
     folder.
  2. Select
    Form 65
    , and go to the
    65 Page 4
    .
  3. Go to the
    Schedule K
     section.
  4. In the
    20C Other Items and Amounts (attach Statement)
    line, the amount must match the following line.
  5. Go to the
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    Alabama
    , and the
    Sch K-1
     folder.
  6. Select
    Partner Name
    , and the sum of all shareholders
    Part III Line Y
     amount.
  7. Verify the values and make necessary changes in the following path.
  8. Go to the
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , and the
    Alabama
     folder.
  9. Select
    State Adjustments
    , and go to
    Other
     section enter the values.
  10. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
note
  • Ensure that the form
    65 Page 4
    ,
    Sch K
    ,
    Line 20C
    ,
    Column C
    amount must equal the total of the
    Sch K-1
    ,
    Part III
    ,
    Line Y
    amount.
  • If you are using the
    K-1 Aggregation
     file, make the necessary changes in the
    K-1 Aggregation
     file and reattach it.
