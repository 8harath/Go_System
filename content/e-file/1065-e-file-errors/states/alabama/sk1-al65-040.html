SK1-AL65-040 e-file error

* SK1-AL65-040: The total of all K-1s, Line W, Column1, Distributions should equal Schedule K, Line 19a, Distributions of cash and marketable securities, Apportioned Amount + Line 19b Distribution of other property, Apportioned Amount.
This happens when Form 65, Page 4, Schedule K, Line 19a and 19b Column c (apportioned amount) doesn’t equal the sum of all Schedules K-1, Line W, Column 1.
Solution
  1. Go to
    Tax forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    Alabama, 65
    , and the
    65, Page 4
     tab.
  2. Verify that the amounts for
    lines 19a and b
     are correct.
  3. Go to
    Sch K-1
    ,
    Name
    , and make that
    line W
     is correct for each partner.
  4. To fix this line, go to the folder
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Alabama
    ,
    State Adjustments
    , and
    Income and Expenses
    .
  5. In the
    Other
     section, enter the correct amount for
    Distribution of cash and marketable securities
     and
    Distribution of other property
    .
  6. If you're using special allocations, enter a special allocation code for each of these items by going to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner information
    , and
    Partner by Partner Data
    .
  7. Select
    Allocations
    ,
    Special Allocations
    , and
    Add new special allocation code
    .
  8. Enter the same allocation code as entered for the item you want to allocate. Then, enter an amount for each partner until 100% of the amount has been allocated.
