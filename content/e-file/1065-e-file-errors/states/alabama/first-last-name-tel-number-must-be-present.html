E-file diagnostic: Person contact first and last name and telephone number must be present

According to AL business rules, person contact first and last name and telephone number must be present. Go to Organizer States Common State General Information Books and Records Contact person tab.
This happens when contact information is missing.

Solution

  • Select
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , then
    Common State
    .
  • Select
    General Information
     then
    Books and Records
    .
  • Enter the first name, last name, and phone number in the Alabama PPT row.
  • Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
