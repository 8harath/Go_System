# E-file diagnostic: General Partner's phone number, and date signed must be present

```
AL E-File - Form PPT: According to AL e-file specifications, General Partner's phone number, and date signed must be present. Go to: Organizer > States > Common State > General Information > Basic Return Information > Signature Information tab
```

This happens when the signature information is missing.

## Solution

- Select **Organizer**, **States**, then the **Common State** folder.
- Under **General Information**, select with **Basic Return Information**.
- Locate the **Signature Information** tab and look for columns I and K for Alabama.
- Enter SSN and Signature date under AL and AL PPT.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
