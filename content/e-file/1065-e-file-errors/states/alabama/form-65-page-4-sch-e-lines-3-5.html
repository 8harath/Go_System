E-file diagnostic: Form 65, Page 4, Schedule E, Lines 3 - 5 must be completed. 

According to AL Business Rules, Form 65, Page 4, Schedule E, Lines 3 - 5 must be completed. If applicable, Lines 1 and 2 must be completed entirely.
You get this error when Question 3 needs an email address from Form 65, Page 4, Schedule E.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
     and select
    Common State
    .
  2. Select
    General Information
     and then
    Basic Return Information
    .
  3. Select the
    Entity Information
     tab and
    Column C
     down menu.
  4. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, then resubmit.
