E-file diagnostic: Form M3, E-mail Address

Form M3, E-mail Address: If you enter a Partnership e-mail address for correspondence, you must select whether this e-mail address belongs to Employee, Paid Preparer, or Other. In addition, if Other is selected, you must fill out the box for Other Description.
This happens when Form M3 Partnership Return Page 2 Email Address for Correspondence has an email address entered but it isn't assigned to someone.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Minnesota
    .
  2. Go to
    General Information
    , then the
    General Information
     section.
  3. Make a selection in
    Partnership email address belongs to
    .
  4. If you select
    Other
    , make sure to enter something in the field provided.
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
