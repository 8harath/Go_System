E-file diagnostic: Form KPC, domestic or foreign address

On Form KPC, partner domestic or foreign address must be significant.
This happens when the Partner Information has missing data such as partner number, 1st name, last name, and address.

Solution

  1. In Organizer, select
    Partner Information
    , then
    Partner by Partner Data
    .
  2. Select
    Columnar Partner Entry
     and verify the information.
  3. Make sure
    Partner Number
     has the complete name and address for all the partners.
  4. Recompute the return and recreate the e-file.
