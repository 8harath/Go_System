Content for field 'ReturnDataState' is incomplete

Solution

  1. Select
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Massachusetts
    , then
    Return Options
    .
  2. Go to the Print Suppression section and clear the
    Form 3K-1
     checkbox.
  3. Do a full re-compute and recreate the e-file.
  4. Select
    States
    ,
    Massachusetts
    ,
    E-file
    ,
    Attachments
    ,
    K-1 Aggregation
    , then
    Summary
    .
  5. Only mark the checkbox if an aggregation file is attached for MA.
