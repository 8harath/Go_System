# E-file diagnostic: MA PTE 63D-ELT, Page 1, line 13 and Page 2, line 1

```
MA PTE 63D-ELT: Page 1 line 13 and Page 2 line 1 must equal the sum of Total Income Subject to 5% Entity Level Tax column for all Qualified Members listed under Organizer / States / Massachusetts / PTE 63D Entity Level Tax, "Qualified Members of Electing Pass-through Entity" section.
```

This happens when the amount on the PTE 63D, Page 1, Line 13 doesn’t match the amount in the Organizer Qualified Members of Electing Pass-through Entity section.

## Solution

- In Organizer, select **Tax Forms**, and the **States** folder.
- Select **Massachusetts**, and the **PTE 63D Entity Level Tax** folder.
- Go to the **PTE 63D Entity Level Tax, Page 1** tab.
- Go to line **13** and make note of the amount.
- In **Organizer**, select the **States** folder.
- Go to **Massachusetts**, then the **PTE 63D Entity Level Tax** folder.
- Go to the grid with the column **Total income Subject to 5.00% entity-level tax**.
- The total of this column needs to match Line **13** (from step 4).
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
