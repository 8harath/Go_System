# E-file diagnostic: Unincorporated business with gross income over $12,000 must file a D-30

```
District of Columbia Unincorporated Business Tax: Generally an unincorporated business with gross income over $12,000 must file a D-30 (whether or not it has net income). This includes any business carrying on or engaging in any trade, business, or commercial activity in DC or receiving income from DC sources. If the partnership meets this criteria and a D-65 is filed instead of the required D-30, attach an explanation to the form D-65.
```

This happens when you file **Form D-65** instead of **Form D-30**.

## Solution

- Go to the 

  **Organizer****States****State and City Activation**

  .
- In the **District of Columbia UBT** row, mark the **Column B** checkbox.
- Go to the 

  **Organizer****States****DC**

   folder.
- Select **General Information**, then go to the **General Information** section.
- Mark the **Form D-30 filed** checkbox.
- Go to **Reason for Filing Form D-65 Instead of Form D-30** section enter the explanation.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
