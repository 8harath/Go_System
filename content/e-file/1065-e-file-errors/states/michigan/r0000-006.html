E-file diagnostic: R0000-006

R0000-006: Form 4946 and Form 4947 must be present.
This happens when you're missing information required by the state. Form 4946 and 4947 is missing from the e-file.
Solution
  1. In Organizer, select the
    States
     folder.
  2. Select
    Michigan
    , then
    Return Options
    .
  3. In the
    Force Print
     section, mark the checkbox for
    Form 4946
    .
  4. Select
    Tax forms
    ,
    States
    , then the
    Michigan
     folder.
  5. Select
    Form 4947
    .
  6. In
    Part 2 Line 28 and Part 3 Line 39
    , enter an amount or
    NONE
    .
  7. Perform a full recompute.
  8. Recreate the e-file.
