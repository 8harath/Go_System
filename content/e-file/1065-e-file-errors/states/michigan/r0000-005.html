Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Michigan
    ,
    E-file
    , then
    Additional Information
    .
  2. Go to the
    E-file Requirement
     section.
  3. Select
    State Stand-Alone Filing
    .
  4. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
