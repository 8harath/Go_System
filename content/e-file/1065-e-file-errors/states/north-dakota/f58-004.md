# F58-004 e-file error

```
F58-004: Total North Dakota withholding on Form 58, line 4 must equal the sum of North Dakota withholding from all 1099-MISC and North Dakota Schedule K-1s submitted with the return.
```

This happens when North Dakota 58, Page 1 Line 4 doesn't match the total of North Dakota withholding from all 1099-MISC and North Dakota Schedule K-1s.

## Solution

- Go to **Tax Forms**, **States**, **North Dakota.**
- The amount for Form 58, 58, Page 1, Line 4 needs to match all 1099-MISC and/or North Dakota Schedule K-1s.
- Go to **Organizer**, **States**, **North Dakota**.
- Select **Passthrough Entity Information**, then **Add New ND Sch K-1 (Received) Info**.
- Complete the information for **ND SCHEDULE K-1 (RECEIVED)**. Make sure that the **North Dakota income tax withheld** equals Line 4 on Form 58.
- Perform a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
