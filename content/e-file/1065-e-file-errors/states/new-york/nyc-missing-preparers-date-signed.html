E-file diagnostic: Missing preparer's date signed 

NYC E-File: Missing preparer's date signed. Go to Organizer New York City General Information 2nd tab Preparer Information
This happens when the preparer’s signature date is missing at the preparer’s information section.

Solution

  • In Organizer, select
    Cities and Intangibles
    .
  • Select
    New York City
    , then
    General Information
    .
  • Select the
    Preparer Information
     tab.
  • In the
    Preparer Information section
    , enter the
    Preparer Date Signed
    .
  • Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
