# E-file diagnostic: NY e-file: According to NY, “C/O” or “In Care of”

```
NY E-File: According to NY, "C/O" or "In Care of" can NOT be entered on the first line of the partnership address. "In Care of" or "C/O" should be entered the 2nd address line. Go to Organizer States.
```

This happens when for the state New York “C/O” or “In Care of” is entered on the 1st line instead of 2nd line partnership address.

## Solution

- Go to **Organizer**, **General information**, **Basic****Return****information** folder.
- Select the **Entity Information** tab.
- Remove **C/O** From **Address Line**.
- Then do a full recompute.
- Recreate the e-file.
