# E-file diagnostic: Form 114.7 Schedule A, Line 1a must equal to Schedule B, Line 9

```
NYC E-File: According to NYC business rules, Form 114.7 Schedule A, Line 1a must equal to Schedule B, Line 9
```

This happens when Form 114.7 Schedule A, line 1a doesn’t equal Schedule B, line 9 usually due to overrides on the form.

## Solution

- Go to the **Tax Forms**, then **Cities and Intangibles** folder.
- Select **New York City**, then select the **114.7** folder.
- Go to the **Line 1a**, if there is an override on that line remove the override.
- Go to the **Override or Imported or Rolled Over 0 Partner Name** section.
- Run a full recompute, recreate the e-file.

note

The detail from Schedule B line 9 will pull to Schedule A, line 1a so if it isn’t populating, then you will have to populate Schedule B to have it transfer.
