E-file diagnostic: IT-204-LL ERO/Preparer Certification or Self Filer Declaration 

IT-204-LL: Must check either ERO/Preparer Certification or Self-Filer Declaration. Both can NOT be selected at the same time. Go to Organizer / States Folder / Electronic Filing - IT-204-LL / Additional Information Screen / Electronic Signature.
This happens when you have checked both ERO/Preparer Certification or Self-Filer Declaration check boxes.

Solution

  1. In Organizer, select
    States
    , then
    New York
    , then
    E-file
    .
  2. Select
    Additional Information
    .
  3. Go to either
    ERO/Preparer Certification and Signature (IT-204)
     or
    Self Filer Declaration and Signature (IT-204)
    section.
  4. Select either one of the options
    I have read the certification above and agree
    .
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
    note
    Both sections cannot have the box checked. Please choose which one you are using ERO or Self Filer and check the box to agree to its terms.
