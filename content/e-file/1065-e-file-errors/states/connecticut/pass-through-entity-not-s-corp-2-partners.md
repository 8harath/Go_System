# E-file error: If the Pass through entity is not a S Corp, Entity must have at least 2 partners

```
If the Pass through entity is not a S Corp, Entity must have at least 2 partners.
```

You need at least two partners if the entity isn't an S Corp.

## Solution

- Go to **Organizer**, **Partner Information**, then **Partner by Partner Data**.
- Select **Add new Partner Name**, and enter partner information.
- Go to **Organizer**, **States**, **Connecticut**, then **Member Information**.
- Enter the correct Partner Codes.
- Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
