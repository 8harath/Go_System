E-file error: If the Pass through entity is not a S Corp, Entity must have at least 2 partners

If the Pass through entity is not a S Corp, Entity must have at least 2 partners.
You need at least two partners if the entity isn't an S Corp.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    , then
    Partner by Partner Data
    .
  2. Select
    Add new Partner Name
    , and enter partner information.
  3. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Connecticut
    , then
    Member Information
    .
  4. Enter the correct Partner Codes.
  5. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
