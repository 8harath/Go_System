CT020-MEF-PART e-file error

Scenario

You may get the following error message when submitting a return.

Error

Invalid or missing CT-NR (CT020-MEF-PART).

Cause

This rejection occurs if you chose the
Alternative Basis
 method for the return, but didn't include Form CT-AB in the e-file.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then
    States
    .
  2. Select
    Connecticut
    , then
    Member Information
    .
  3. Enter an amount in
    Total Connecticut source income included in return (Override)
    .
    note
    If there's no
    Connecticut source income
     on the return, enter
    NONE
    .
  4. Do a
    Full Recompute
    , re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
