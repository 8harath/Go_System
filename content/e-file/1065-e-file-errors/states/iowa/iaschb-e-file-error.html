IASchB e-file error

Field 'IASchB' is unexpected

Solution

  1. Select
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , then
    Iowa
    .
  2. Select
    Penalties and Interest
    , then
    Overrides
    .
  3. Enter
    NONE
     on
    Penalties and interest
     or Line item
    Overpayment carryforward from prior period
    .
  4. Do a full recompute, recreate the e-file, then resubmit.
