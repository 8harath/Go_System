  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Iowa
    .
  2. In the
    Iowa
     folder, select
    A and A/Business Activity Ratio

    Business Activity Ratio
     look for the
    Other Types of Income
     section.

  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
