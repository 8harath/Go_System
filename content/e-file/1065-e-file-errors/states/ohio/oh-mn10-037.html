OH-MN10-037 e-file error

Error

The historical credit carryforward amount for the municipal corporation indicated cannot exceed the tax liability regardless of the total carryforward amount available for the current taxable year. Remaining carryforward amounts will be available for use in preceeding taxable years. Please correct and retransmit.(OH-MNP10-037)

Cause

The historical Credit Carryforward can't be greater than the Tax Liability.

Solution

Review the consolidated totals line:
  1. In the Tax Forms, select
    Cities
    .
  2. Select
    Ohio Generic Cities
    ,
    OH
    , then
    Ohio MNP
    .
  3. Review the Consolidated totals line on Form MNP10, Page 3.
note
The historical Credit Carryforward can't be greater than the Tax Liability. This is an e-file limitation imposed by the state of Ohio. Please reach out to the jurisdiction for further clarification.
