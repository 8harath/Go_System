OH-PTE-022 e-file error

The taxpayer has not verified the current address on the return. VerifiedAddress must be True. Please correct and retransmit.
This happens when the
Has address been verified?
 field isn't completed.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Ohio
    ,
    E-file
    , and then
    Additional Information
    .
  2. In the
    IT-4708 or IT-1140 section
    , enter X in the
    Has address been verified?
     field.
  3. Perform a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
