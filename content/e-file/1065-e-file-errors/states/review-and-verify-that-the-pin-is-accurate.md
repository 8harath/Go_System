# E-file diagnostic: Please review and verify that the PIN is accurate

```
Common State E-file Alert: Please review and verify that the PIN is accurate and that the preparer is the registered owner of the PTIN.
```

This happens when the PIN is inaccurate or the preparer isn’t the registered owner of the PTIN.

## Solution

- In **Tax Forms**, go to **States**.
- elect **Common State** then **General Information**.
- Select **Paid Preparer Information**, then go to **Preparer Information** tab.
- In **Common State** row, **PTIN** column enter the valid **PTIN** number.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
