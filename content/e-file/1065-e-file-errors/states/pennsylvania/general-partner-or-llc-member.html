E-file diagnostic: General Partner or LLC Member 

Missing required general partner's first name, last name, title and/or signature date.
This diagnostic occurs when some information is missing such as first name, last name and signature.
Solution
  1. In Organizer, select
    States
    , then
    Common State
    .
  2. Select
    General Information
     then the
    Basic Return Information
    folder.
  3. On the
    Signature Information
     tab, enter the first name, last name, and the signature date for Pennsylvania.
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate e-file.
