PA20-S/PA-65-009 e-file error

PA20-S/PA-65-009 (PA20S\PA-65, Page 3, Part IX): Business Name Line 1 must be present, left justified and must not contain any special characters (. # \ & / , ') other than a hyphen or more than one consecutive space. See XXXXX.
The business name is what causes the error is in the error message.

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    Activity Information
    Pass-Through Entity
    .
  2. Select the partner's name, then
    Pass - Through Entity Activity
    .
  3. On the
    K-1 Line, Lines 1-15
     tab, remove any special characters in the
    Pass-Through name (Mandatory)
     field.
  4. Do a full recompute and re-create the e-file.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then
    States
    , and select
    Pennsylvania
    .
  2. Select
    Schedule E
    .
  3. Go to the section
    Ownership of Pass-Thru Entities
     and make sure there are no special characters in the
    Name (override)
     column.
  4. Do a full recompute and re-create the e-file.

Solution 3

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then
    States
    , and select
    Common State
    .
  2. Select
    General Information
    , then
    Basic Return Information
    .
  3. On the
    Address
     tab, go to the Pennsylvania row and make sure there aren't special characters in the
    Name
     column B.
  4. Do a full recompute and re-create the e-file.
