E-file error: Field 'SectionII' cannot be empty

Field 'SectionII' cannot be empty
You'll get this error if Line C of Schedule NW doesn't have an amount.

Solution

  1. Select
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , then
    Pennsylvania
    .
  2. Select
    Return Options
    .
  3. Go to the Print Suppression section and mark the checkbox for
    Schedule NW
    .
  4. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
