PA20-S/PA-65-004 e-file error

PA20-S/PA-65-004 efile reject: Rent/Royalty Net Income (Loss) Line 6, Column A or B on the PA20S/65 are present and Schedule E is not included with the filing.
This happens when the Rent and Royalty Net Income (Loss) Line 6, Column A or B on the PA20S/65 are present, and Schedule E is not included with the filing.
Solution
  1. Go to the
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Pennsylvania
    folder.
  2. Select
    Schedule E
    .
  3. Go to the
    Rent or Royalty Income (loss) From Other Entities
     section.
  4. In line
    Rent or Royalty Income (loss) from PA Sources (Override)
    and
    Rent or Royalty Income (loss) from Outside PA Sources (Override)
    enter the values or
    NONE
    .
  5. Do a full Recompute.
  6. Recreate the E-file.
