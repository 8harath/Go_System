E-file error: Field 'SchCP' is unexpected

Field 'SchCP' is unexpected
You'll get this error when Pennsylvania expects a Form 65-Corp instead of a PA-20S/65 Information return.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Pennsylvania
    ,
    E-file PA20S/PA-65
    , then
    Forms Included in E-file
    .
  2. Go to the
    Forms Included in E-file
     section.
  3. Clear the
    PA-20S/65: PA S Corporation/Partnership Information Return
     checkbox.
  4. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
