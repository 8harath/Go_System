E-file error: TotRentBusIncApp

The field 'TotRentBusIncApp' with value '-28', data format is not correct.
This happens when the you need negative numbers aren't allowed.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    Pennsylvania
    , then
    Schedule M
    .
  2. Select
    Schedule M - Rent and Royalty - Col F tab, Section III
    .
  3. Ensure
    Line 8
     isn't a negative number. The line can't be in a negative format. It needs to be positive number to E-file or qualify the return.
