NCD403-1380 e-file error

NCD403-1380: [D-403] If Part 4 Section C Line 18 is populated with a non-zero value, Part 4 Line 20 must be equal to Part 4 Line 18 minus Part 4 Line 19; otherwise Part 4 Section C Line 20 must be zero or blank.
This happens when Part 4 Section C Line 18 is populated with a non-zero value. Part 4 Line 20 needs to be equal to Part 4 Line 18 minus Part 4 Line 19, otherwise Part 4 Section C Line 20 needs to be zero or blank.

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    , then select
    States
    , then
    North Carolina
    .
  2. Select
    Form D-403
    , then go to Page 3.
  3. Select
    Partner Information
    .
  4. If you have an amount in line 18, then line 20 should equal line 18 minus the line 19 amount.
  5. To populate line 20, in Organizer, select
    States
    , then
    North Carolina
    .
  6. Select
    Nonresident Partner Affirmation
    .
  7. In the Print Suppression section, uncheck
    Form NC-NPAs Nonresident Partner Affirmation (does not alter Part 4 tax calculations)
    .
  8. Perform a full recompute and recreate the e-file.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then select
    States
    , then
    North Carolina
    .
  2. Select
    State Adjustments
  3. In the Overrides section, enter an
    Amount
     and
    Allocation Code
     for Total tax due for nonresident partners.
  4. Perform a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
