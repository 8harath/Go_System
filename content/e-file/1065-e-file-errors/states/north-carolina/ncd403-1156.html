NCD403-1156 e-file error

NCD403-1156: [D-403] Part 1 Line 14b must be equal to the sum of the following lines from all the associated forms received by the partnership: Partnership (D403) NC-K1 Line 9.
This happens when North Carolina Form D-403, D-403, Line 14b doesn't equal "Net Tax Due" in Tax Forms.

Solution

  1. Review and copy the amount on line 14b in
    Tax Forms
    States
    North Carolina
    D-403
    D-403, Page 2
    .
  2. Go to
    Organizer
    , then
    States
    .
  3. Select
    North Carolina
    ,
    Additional K-1 Information
    , then
    Other Partnerships
    .
  4. Add a new NCK-1 from Partnership.
  5. Populate this area because it's associated with line 14b of Form D-403.
  6. Make sure that the total for
    Net Tax Due
     and
    Share Tax Withheld
     amounts match the amount on Line 14b.
  7. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
