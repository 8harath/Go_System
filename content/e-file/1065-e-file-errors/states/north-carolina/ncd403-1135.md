# NCD403-1135 e-file error

```
NCD403-1135: [D-403] Part 1 Line 9 must equal Part 1 Line 7 minus Part 1 Line 8.
```

This happens when Form D-403, Page 1, Line 9 doesn’t equal line 7 minus line 8.

## Solution

- In Organizer, select **States**, then **North Carolina**.
- Select **State adjustments** and go to the Overrides section.
- The amount for **Net distributive partnership income: To be apportioned to North Carolina** needs to be equal to the amount on Form D-403, Page 1, line 7-line 8. If it's not, then the amount will override Form D-403, Page 1, line 9 and then the amount won't be equal.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
