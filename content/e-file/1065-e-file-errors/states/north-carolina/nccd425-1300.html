NCCD425-1300 e-file error

NCCD425-1300 - S: [CD-425] Line 24 must equal CD-405 Schedule B line 23 or CD-401S Schedule B line 19.
This happens when you need a value on line 8 for form CD-425.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    North Carolina
    ,
    CD-425 Tax Credit Summary
    , and then
    CD-425 Tax Credit Summary
    .
  2. If there isn't a value enter
    NONE
     on line 8.
  3. Do a recompute and re-create the e-file
