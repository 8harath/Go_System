NCD403-1340 e-file error

Error

[D-403] If Part 4 Section B is present, Part 4 Line 17 must be equal to the sum of Part 4 Lines 13, 14, 15, and 16. (NCD403-1340)

Cause

North Carolina Form D-403, Page 2, Part 4, Line 17 needs to equal the total of lines 13-16.

Solution

  1. In the Tax Forms, select
    States
     then
    North Carolina
    .
  2. Go to Form D-403, then D-403, Page 2.
  3. In Part 4, select
    Partner Information
    .
  4. For all partners, make sure that line 17 equals the total of lines 13-16. If there is no amount, enter
    NONE
    .
  5. Do a full recompute, recreate the electronic file, and resubmit the return.
