NCD403-1155 e-file error

* NCD403-1155: [D-403] If election to be a Taxed Partnership is "Yes", Part 1 Line 13 must equal the sum of Part 4 Line 20 from the Partners Total Column and Part 4 Line 27 from the Partners Total Col
This happens when in state North Carolina Form D-403 Part 1 line 13 is not equal to the sum of Part 4 Line 20 from the Partners Total Column and Part 4 Line 27 from the Partners Total Col.

Solution

  1. In Tax Forms, select
    States
    , then
    North Carolina
    .
  2. Form D-403 Page 2 line 13 should be the same as Form D-403 Page 3 line 20.
  3. Perform a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
