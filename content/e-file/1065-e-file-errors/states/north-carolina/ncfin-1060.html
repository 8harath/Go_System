NCFIN-1060 e-file error

This error happens during North Carolina 1065 extension e-file when payment contact information is missing or when overrides on Form D-410P Lines 1 and 2 cause a mismatch between the calculated balance due and the bank information payment amount.

Error message

NCFIN-1060: [Financial] Payment contact name and phone number must be provided

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Common State
    General Information
    Bank Information
    .
  2. Confirm you've entered the payment contact name and phone number fields.
  3. Go to
    Tax Forms
    States
    North Carolina
    D-410P
    .
  4. Check Lines
    1
     and
    2
     for overrides. Overrides on these fields cause incorrect balance due calculations
  5. Clear the overrides on Lines
    1
     and
    2
    .
  6. Confirm the balance due on Line
    3
     (Total amount due) matches the payment amount entered on the bank information screen.
  7. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
  8. If you still have the diagnostic, go to
    Organizer
    States
    Common State
    General Information
    Basic Information
    Signature Information
    .
  9. Make sure the
    Name
     and
    Telephone number
     is filled out.
  10. You can pay when you e-file your return or extension. For more information, visit the NC Department of Revenue website.
