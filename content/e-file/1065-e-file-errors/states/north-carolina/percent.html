E-file error: Field 'Percent' with value '100.0001', data format is not correct

Form: FormNCD403[1] Part4[1]/PartnersTotal[1]/PartA[1]/PercentOwnership[1]/Percent[1]
Error: The field 'Percent' with value '100.0001', data format is not correct.
The Partner's share percentage (total) should not be over 100% and also can't be negative or none.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
     and select
    North Carolina
    .
  2. Select
    D-403
    ,
    D-403
    ,
    Page 3
    ,
    Part 4
    ,
    Line 4
     -  The Partner's share percentage (total) should not be over 100% and also can't be negative or none.
  3. If there is no partner share percentage, 0 and none work, Spacebar overriding will allow the return to qualify.
  4. Select
    Partner Information
     and correct partners' share percentage.
  5. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, then resubmit.
