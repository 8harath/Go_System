NCD403-1140 e-file error

NCD403-1140: [D-403] If Part 3 is present, then Part 1 Line 10 must equal Part 3 Line 2; otherwise, Part 1 Line 10 must be zero or blank.
This happens when Form D-403, Page 2 Part 3 Line 2 has an amount then Nonapportionable Net Distributive Partnership Income (Loss) requires an explanation.

Solution

  1. In Tax Forms, select
    States
    , then
    North Carolina
    .
  2. Select
    D-403
    , then
    D-403, Page 2
    , then
    Part 3 Nonapportionable Net Distributive Partnership Income (Loss) requires an Explanation
    .
  3. In Organizer, select
    States
    , then
    North Carolina
    , then
    State Adjustments
    .
  4. Select
    Nonapportionable Net Distributive Partnership Income
    .
  5. Enter an explanation where it states Explanation for why income is listed as nonapportionable income; rather than apportionable income.
  6. Perform a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
