NCD403-1180 e-file error

NCD403-1180: [D-403] If Part 1 Line 20 is greater than Part 1 Line 21d, then Part 1 Line 23 must be equal to Part 1 Line 20 minus Part 1 Line 21d; otherwise Part 1 Line 23 must be zero or blank.
You'll get this error if Line 23 contains NONE.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    North Carolina
    ,
    D-403
    , then the
    D-403, Page 2
     tab.
  2. Make sure Line 23 is clear and doesn't include
    NONE
    . Remove the source lines causing the NONE calculation, specifically the values prior to Line 20.
  3. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    North Carolina
    , then
    State Adjustments
    .
  4. Go to the
    Overrides
     section.
  5. Enter an amount in the
    Total tax due for nonresident partners
     field.
  6. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
