E-file error: Field 'PartnersTotal' is unexpected

Field 'PartnersTotal' is unexpected
You'll get this error if you mark the
Suppress partner information
 checkbox.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    North Carolina
    ,
    E-file
    ,
    Attachments
    ,
    K-1 Aggregation
    , then
    Summary
    .
  2. Clear the
    Suppress partner information
     checkbox.
  3. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
