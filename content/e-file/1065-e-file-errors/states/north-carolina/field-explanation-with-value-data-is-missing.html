E-file error: The field 'Explanation' with value '', data is missing

The field 'Explanation' with value '', data is missing.
This error displays if you didn't enter an explanation for nonapportionable income.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    North Carolina
    , then
    State Adjustments
    .
  2. Go to the Nonapportionable Net Distributive Partnership Income section.
  3. Enter information for
    Explanation of why income is listed is nonapportionable income; rather than apportionable income
    .
  4. Do a full recompute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
