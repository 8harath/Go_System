43158 e-file error

TotPartIncColAAllSource amount invalid
You may get this error if Lines 19a and 22a have different amounts.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    New Jersey
    ,
    NJ-1065
    , then
    NJ-1065, Page 2
    .
  2. Review line 19, Column A and line 22, column A. They must match.
  3. To update amounts:
    1. Go to
      Organizer
      States
      New Jersey
      .
    2. Select
      Federal Overrides
      .
    3. In the
      Income And Subtractions
       section, enter the amount and allocation code in
      22 Partnership Income (loss) override
  4. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
