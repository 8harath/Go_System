43128 e-file error

43128: NJ 1065 Page 2 Line 7 does not match Schedule A Column C Line 7

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
     and select
    States
    , then
    New Jersey
    .
  2. Select
    Schedule A Information
    .
  3. In Column B - INCOME EARNED BY OTHER PARTNERSHIP INCOME, enter
    NONE
     for line 7, Royalty Income.
  4. Do a full re-compute and re-create e-file.
