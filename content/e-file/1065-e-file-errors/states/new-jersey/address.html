E-file error: Field 'Address' cannot be empty

Form: SchPD[1] PartnersDirect[45]/Address[1]
Error: Address
ActionNeeded: Field 'Address' cannot be empty
You'll get this error if a partner's address information is blank.

Solution

  1. Select
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    , then
    Partner by Partner Data
    .
  2. Select
    Partner Information
    , then
    Partner Information
     again.
  3. Make sure all partners have an address, state, city, and ZIP Code.
  4. Do a full recompute, recreate the e-file, then resubmit.
