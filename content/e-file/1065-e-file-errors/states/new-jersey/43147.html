43147 e-file error

* 43147: Schedule NJK-1 Part I, Date Partner's Interest in Partnership began must be present.
This happens when you haven’t entered the Date interest began in New Jersey for the partners.

Solution 1

  1. In
    Organizer
    , select
    Partner Information
    , then the
    Partner by Partner Data
     folder.
  2. Select
    Columnar Partner Entry
    , then the
    State Information
     tab.
  3. Go to the
    State Information for Partners
     section, select the
    New Jersey
     hyperlink.
  4. Go to the
    Partners' State Information - Maryland to New Mexico
     section.
  5. In the
    Date Partner's Int Began in NJ
     column, enter the date for all partners.
  6. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.

Solution 2

  1. In
    Organizer
    , select
    Partner Information
    , then the
    Partner by Partner Data
     folder.
  2. Select the
    Partner Name
     folder.
  3. Select
    Partner Information
    , then go to the
    State Partner Information
     tab.
  4. Go to the
    New Jersey
     section.
  5. Enter the date in Line
    Date interest began in New Jersey
    .
  6. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
