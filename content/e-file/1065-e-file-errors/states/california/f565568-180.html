F565/568-180 e-file error

CA Rule F565/568-180: If Line 8 amount is present, then Schedule D-1 must have data.
This happens when California Form 565 Side 1 Line 8 Total gains included on Schedule D-1, Part II Line 17 (gain only) is populated but Schedule D-1 information is missing.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    , then
    States
    .
  2. Select
    California
    , then go to
    Form 565, Side 1, Line 8
    .
  3. If Line 8 is populated, then the Schedule D-1 must be populated. Line 8 should be equal to all the gains in Schedule D-1.
    1. To populate Schedule D-1, go to
      Organizer
      and
      Gains and Losses
      .
    2. Select
      Sales of Business Property
      and
      Property Descriptions
      .
    3. Enter details for the property. If you need to add a new property, select
      Add new Description of Property
      .
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
