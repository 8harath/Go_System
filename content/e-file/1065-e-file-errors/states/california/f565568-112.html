F565/568-112 e-file error

CA Rule F565/568-112: If Form 565 Question BB or Form 568 Question Z is checked "Yes", then Schedule EO Part II must have data. Navigation: Organizer>States>California>Other State Forms>Schedule EO.
This happens when California Form 565, Side 3 Question BB or Form 568, Side 2 Line Z is checked "Yes" and Schedule EO Part II is empty.

Solution 1

If you didn’t mean to answer Form 565 Question BB or Form 568 Question Z with Yes, you can change your answer.
  1. In Organizer, select
    States
    , then
    California
    , then
    Additional Information
    .
  2. Select
    Questions
    , then BB/Z Does this partnership/LLC own interest in a business entity disregarded for tax purposes?
  3. Change you answer to
    No
    .
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file

Solution 2

If you need to add information to Schedule EO Part II,
  1. In Tax Forms, select
    States
    , then
    California
    .
  2. Select
    568
    , then
    568
    , then
    568, Side 2, line Z
    .
  3. If
    Does this LLC own an interest in a business entity disregarded for tax purposes?
     is
    Yes
    , complete Schedule EO, Part II.
  4. Complete Schedule EO in Organizer.
  5. Select
    States
    California
    Other State Forms
    Schedule EO
    .
  6. Part I - Partial Ownership of Less than 100%
     and
    Part II – Full Ownership of 100%
     must have at least one entry with all the information filled out.
  7. Do a full-recompute and recreate the e-file.
