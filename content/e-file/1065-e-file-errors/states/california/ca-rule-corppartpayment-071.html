E-file error: CA Rule CorpPartPayment-071

CA Rule CorpPartPayment-071: If filing Form 3893 PTE Tax, Entity type must be present. Navigation: Tax Form>States>California>Form 565 Side 2 Item J.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    State E-file
    , then
    Enable/Create Returns
    .
  2. In Column C, clear the checkboxes for
    California PTE TAX 1
     and
    California PTE TAX 2
    .
  3. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
