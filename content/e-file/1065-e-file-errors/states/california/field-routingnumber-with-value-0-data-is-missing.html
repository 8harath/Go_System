E-file error: The field 'RoutingNumber' with value '0', data is missing

The field 'RoutingNumber' with value '0', data is missing
You'll get this error if the routing number field is blank or contains a 0.

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Common State
    ,
    General Information
    ,
    Bank Information
    , then the
    Direct Debit
     tab.
  2. Enter all information for line
    California
    .
  3. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Common State
    ,
    General Information
    , Basic Return Information, then the Signature Information tab.
  4. Enter a phone number in column H.
  5. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    California
    ,
    Disregarded Entity
    , then
    Tax and Fee Calculation
    .
  2. Go to the Tax Calculation and Overrides section for California.
  3. Mark
    Check if not subject to LLC tax of $800
    .
  4. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
