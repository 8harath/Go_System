X0000-010 e-file error

Error

Your State submission does not match the latest published FTB Schema or is not well formed. You must contact your software provider to resolve this error.

Cause

This return has missing or incorrect partner information entered for disregarded entities.

Solutions

/CA-Return/CA-ReturnData/CAForm565ScheduleK-1/PartnerInformation/DisregardedEntity/NotApplicable
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    California
    , and then
    Partner Information
    .
  2. Review partner information to ensure it's accurate. If a partner is designated as a disregarded entity, then you need to complete the
    Disregarded Entity
     section.
  3. Perform a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
/CA-Return/CA-ReturnData/CAForm565ScheduleK-1/PartnerInformation/DisregardedEntity/TIN
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    , and then
    Partner by Partner Data
    .
  2. Review any partner designated as a disregarded entity and verify the TIN is accurate.
  3. Perform a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
