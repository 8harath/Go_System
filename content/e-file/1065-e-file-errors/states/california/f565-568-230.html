F565/568-230 e-file error

CA Rule F565/568-230: If Line 17a amount is present, then FTB 3885P/3885L must have data.
Form 565/568: Form 565, Side 1/Form 568, Side 4 Line 17a Depreciation and Amortization amount must match Form 3885P/3885L Line 6 amount.

Solution

Go to
Tax Forms
States
California
 and review Form 565 or 568, line 17a. If there is any amount on this line, Form 3885 needs to calculate and line 6 needs an amount.
  1. On Form 565 or 568, select the drill down on Line 17a and clear any overrides on the yellow workpaper.
  2. Go to
    Organizer
     and select
    Ordinary Income and Deductions
    .
  3. Select
    Depreciation and Amortization
     then the entity name.
  4. In the
    4562 Information
     folder, select
    4562 Overrides
    .
  5. Select the link for
    State Overrides
     and there is a section you can enter information for
    California Only
    . Populate here to generate the Form 3885P.
  6. Finally, the amount on Line 17a must match Form 3885P/3885L Line 6 amount.
