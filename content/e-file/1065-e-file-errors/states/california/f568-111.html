F568-111 e-file error

If Form 565 Question Q or Form 568 Question S is checked "Yes", then Schedule EO Part I must have data and ownership must be less than 100%.
If you don't answer
Yes
 for Sch EO Part II Question Z or S, Schedule EO Part II won't print.

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    California
    ,
    Other State Forms
    , then
    Schedule EO
    .
  2. For Pass-Through Entity Ownership, enter Sch EO information for both Part I and Part II.
  3. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    California
    ,
    Additional Information
    , then
    Questions
    .
  2. Select
    No
     for Line Q/S.
  3. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.

Solution 3

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    California
    ,
    Additional Information
    , then
    Questions
    .
  2. Select
    Yes
     for Line BB/Z,
    Does this partnership/LLC own an interest in a business entity disregarded for tax purposes?
  3. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
