F565-130 e-file error

CA Rule F565-130: Form 565 Line 2, amount must equal Schedule A Line 8 amount.
This happens when Form 565 Schedule A Line 8 doesn’t equal Schedule B Line 2.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    , and select
    California
    ,
    568
    ,
    568, Side 4
    .
  2. Schedule A
    , Line 8 needs to match
    Schedule B
    , Line 2.
  3. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , and select
    California, Federal Overrides
    ,
    Ordinary Income and Deductions
    .
  4. Go to
    Income Summary
    , then the
    Cost of Goods Sold
    tab.
  5. Enter an amount for line 2.
  6. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
