X0000-010 e-file error xpath /CA-Return/CA-ReturnData/CAForm 565ScheduleK-1/PartnerInformation/Individual

Error

Your State submission does not match the latest published FTB Schema or is not well formed. You must contact your software provider to resolve this error.

Cause

This return has missing or incorrect partner information.

Solutions

/CA-Return/CA-ReturnData/CAForm565ScheduleK-1/PartnerInformation/Individual/
IdNumber
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    ,
    Partner by Partner Data
    , then
    Columnar Partner Entry
    .
  2. Review any partner with
    Individual
     as the entity type. These partners need to have Social Security Number (SSN) and not an Employe Identification Number (EIN).
  3. Perform a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit. 
/CA-Return/CA-ReturnData/CAForm565ScheduleK-1/PartnerInformation/Individual/

LastName
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    ,
    Partner by Partner Data
    , then
    Columnar Partner Entry
    .
  2. Go to the
    Reformat Partner Name for State Purpose
     tab and remove any special characters from the partners' names, including the middle initial.
    note
    1. The last name can't be more than 17 characters.
    2. When using a K-1 Aggregation file, the partner's name needs to be reformatted into first and last name used to create the K-1 file. Re-attach the file to the locator.
  3. Perform a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
