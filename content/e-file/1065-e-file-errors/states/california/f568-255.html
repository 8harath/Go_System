F568-255 e-file error

Scenario

You may get the following error message when submitting a return.

Error

Schedule B, Line 23 [OrdinaryIncomeLoss] must equal the result of Line 12 [TotalIncomeLoss] minus Line 22 [TotalDeductions]. (F568-255).

Cause

Ordinary income
 (Line 23) on
Schedule B
 doesn't equal
Total income
 (Line 12) minus
Total deductions
 (Line 22).

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    and select
    States
    .
  2. Select
    California
    , then
    568
    .
  3. Select
    568
    , then
    568, Side 4
    .
  4. Remove any overrides from
    Schedule B
    .
  5. Do a
    Full Recompute
    , re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
