F565-067 e-file error

CA Rule F565-067: Form 565, Side 1 Total asset at EOY must have data unless Side 2, Line P is checked "Yes".
This happens when you don’t have a total asset value on Form 565 Side 1 or you're missing an entry on Form 565 Side 2 Line P.
Solution
Use the following steps to answer Form 565 Side 2 Line P with Yes.
  1. Go to the
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    California
    , and then the
    Additional Information
     folder.
  2. Select
    Questions
    , and go to the
    Questions
     section.
  3. Go to
    Line P
    ,
    Does the partnership meet all the requirements shown in the instructions for Question P?
     and select the radio button
    YES
    .
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
Solution 2
If your asset information is correct, and you don’t want to select
Yes
, then you need to paper file the return.
