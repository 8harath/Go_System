# E-file diagnostic: Form 568 Schedule T: Tax due

```
Form 568 Schedule T: When using California 568 Schedule T aggregation, Form 568 Side 4 Schedule T Total tax due must have amount populated and must equal the sum of all Col G Net tax due from aggregation file
```

This happens when Form 568, Side 4, Schedule T, Total amount of tax due is incorrect.

## Solution

- Go to **Tax forms**, **States**, **California**, **568** and select **568**.
- Select the **568, Side 4** tab.
- In the **Schedule T** section, make a note of the total amount of tax due.
- Select the **Detail** link.
- Verify that the **Net Tax Due** column hasn't been overridden for any partners.
- The **total** for the **Net Tax Due** column should be equal to what is on **Schedule T, Total tax due**.

If a partner is showing in error, use the following steps.

- Go to **Organizer**, **Partner by Partner information**, **Name of partner**, and then **Partner information**.
- Select the **State information** tab.
- In the **California** section, deselect **Nonconsenting nonresident who did not sign Form 3832**.
