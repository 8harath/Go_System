E-file error: Field 'ForeignAddress' is unexpected

Field 'ForeignAddress' is unexpected

Solution

  1. Select
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    , then
    Partner by Partner Data
    .
  2. Select the partner name, then the
    Partner Information
     tab.
  3. Go to the SSN/EIN section.
  4. Go to
    Utah
     and enter the
    Affiliate Date
    .
  5. Select
    SSN
     and enter a SSN in the correct format (XXX-XX-XXXX).
  6. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
