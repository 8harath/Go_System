E-file error: Federal attachments for Idaho Form 65

Federal Attachments: The State of Idaho requires a complete copy of Federal Form 1065 and Schedule K-1s for all partners/members to be attached when filing Idaho Form 65.
This happens when there's missing federal attachments in the Idaho state e-file.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then
    States
    .
  2. Select
    Common State
    , then
    Print Options
    .
  3. Select
    Print a complete federal return as an attachment for requiring states and cities
    .
  4. Do a full re-compute and recreate the e-file.
