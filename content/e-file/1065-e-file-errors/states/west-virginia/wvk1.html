E-file error: Field 'WVK1' is unexpected

Field 'WVK1' is unexpected
You need to have at least 1 partner with a partner number.

Solution

  1. Select
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    , then
    Partner by Partner Data
    .
  2. Add a partner with a partner number.
  3. Do a full recompute, recreate the e-file, then resubmit.
To exclude this partner from the e-file, follow these steps:
  1. Select
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    ,
    Common Partner Data
    , then the
    Sch K-1 Print Options
     tab.
  2. Go to the Print Options section and select the
    E-file Only: Suppress...
     link.
  3. Mark the partner you want to exclude.
