# Federal e-file status requested

```
Federal e-file status requested.
```

You get this error during the IRS shutdown.

## Solution

Each year the IRS shuts down in December to prepare for the upcoming filing season and re-opens in January.

All returns submitted during the IRS shutdown will be received by Thomson Reuters and will have an electronic postmark at the time of receipt. These returns won't be submitted to the IRS and state agencies until the IRS re-opens in January.

note

This annual shutdown doesn't extend the IRS 10-day transmission perfection period.
