F1065-037-02 e-file error

If Form 1065, Schedule B, Line 15 has a non-zero value, then 1 or more Forms 8865 must be present in the return.
This happens when Form 1065, Schedule B, Line 15 indicates a Form 8865 is attached, but there isn't an attachment to the e-file.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    General Information
    , and then
    Questions
    .
  2. Find question 15. Review your data entry.
    1. If no Form 8865 needs to be attached, remove an amount entered in this field. You'll also want to review data entry in
      Organizer
      ,
      Federal E-file
      ,
      International
      , and then
      Third Party XML
      . Review the
      Form 8865
       section and the
      Number of Forms 8865 attached through XML
       field.
    2. If 1 or more Form 8865 needs to be attached, attach the form to the e-file.
  3. Perform a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
