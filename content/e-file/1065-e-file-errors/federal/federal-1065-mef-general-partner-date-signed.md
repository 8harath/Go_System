# E-file diagnostic: Federal 1065 MeF: General Partner Date Signed must have a value

```
Federal 1065 MeF: General Partner Date Signed must have a value. Organizer General Information Return Information Signing General Partner or LLC Member Manager enter the General Partner or LLC Member Signature Date.
```

This happens when fields are incomplete for signing partner or LLC.

## Solution

- Go to 

  **Organizer****General Information****Basic Return information**

  .
- Select the **Return Information** tab.
- In the **Signing Partner or LLC Member** section, complete all fields, including **First name**, **Last name**, **Title**, and **Date**.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
