F1065-048-02 e-file error

If Form 1065, Schedule B, Line 4 'PrtshpSatisfyAllConditionsInd' is "No", then Schedule L must be present in the return.
This happens when Question 4 in Schedule B is set to
No
, and the Schedule L is missing.

Solution

If Question 4 is incorrect:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then
    General Information
    .
  2. Select
    Questions
    , then the
    Schedule B Questions
     tab.
  3. Set Question 4 to
    No
     or select
    Blank
    . This action will prompt you to complete Schedule L.
  4. In Schedule L, enter an amount or
    NONE
    .
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
