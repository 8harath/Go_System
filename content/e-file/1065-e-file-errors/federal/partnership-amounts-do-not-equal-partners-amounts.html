E-file diagnostic: Partnership amounts for Income do not equal total of partners amounts 

Partnership amounts for Income do not equal total of partners' amounts: Please check the Reconciliation of Partnership to Partner Amounts.
This e-file error will show if the federal Partnership amounts for Income don’t equal total of partners' amounts or blank.

Solution 1

Enable Partner Allocations:
  1. In Organizer, check if
    Partner Allocations
     are enabled. If not, select the checkbox for
    Enable Partner Allocations
    .
  2. Do a full recompute to apply the changes.

Solution 2

Verify Override Amounts in Schedule K:
  1. Go to Organizer then
    Sch K
    .
  2. Make sure any overridden amounts match the amounts on Form 1065 Schedule K.
  3. Check Tax Forms for any overrides that affect the Schedule K.
  4. Do a full recompute to apply any changes.

Solution 3

Review Income Summary for Allocation Codes:
  1. In Organizer go to
    Sch K
    , then
    Income Summary
     and open
    Income summary
    .
  2. If an allocation code for
    Ordinary Income
     has been entered read the associated warning for this item.
  3. Check for Over-Allocation in Income and Deduction Summary:
    1. Go to Organizer then
      Sch K
      .
    2. Select
      Income Summary
       then
      Deduction Summary
    3. Make sure Allocation Codes haven’t been entered for total amounts and detail items that could cause overallocation.
      note
      • Don't allocate Other Interest income on the Income Summary tab.
      • Don't allocate detail lines for Other Interest Income on the Income Other Interest tab.
  4. Do a full recompute to apply any changes.
