E-file error: Field 'TaxYearBeginDt' is unexpected

Field 'TaxYearBeginDt' is unexpected

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then
    Foreign Information
    .
  2. Select
    Ptr Distributive Share-International
    , then
    General Information On PFIC or QEF-VII
    .
  3. Select
    Per Partner
    , then
    PFIC Information
     and enter address information.
  4. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, then resubmit.
