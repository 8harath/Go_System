E-file error: The field 'CountryCd' with value 'X', data format is not correct.

The field 'CountryCd' with value 'X', data format is not correct.

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Foreign Information
    ,
    Foreign Corporation
    , and select the partner name.
  2. Go to
    Schedule Q
    ,
    GEN
    ,
    Tested Income Group
    , then
    Schedule Q, Line 3 - Tested Income Group
    .
  3. Select the country.
  4. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    Federal
    ,
    Direct K-1 Import
    , and select the partner name.
  2. Select
    Import K1 Partner Information
     and enter the correct country code.
  3. Check the partner's country code and foreign address information from the XML data imported.
  4. Reimport the Direct K-1.
  5. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
