F1065-088 e-file error

Scenario

You may get the following error message if both Form 1065 AmendedReturnind and Form 8082, BBAAARind are selected, then Schedule K-1 (Form 1065) must not be present in the return.
Error message
If both Form 1065 AmendedReturnind and Form 8082, BBAAARind are selected, then Schedule K-1 (Form 1065) must not be present in the return.

Solution

  1. Go the
    Organizer
    , then
    General Information
    , then
    Return and Print Options
    .
  2. Select
    Sch K, K-1 and Activity Schedules
     tab.
  3. In the
    Print or Suppress
     section, select
    Suppress
     for
    Schedule K-1, partner activity schedules
    ....
  4. Then select
    Tax Forms
    , then
    Federal
    .
  5. Select
    1065 - US Partnership Return of Income
    , then
    Page 1
    .
  6. Go to Item 1 and override
    Full Recompute
    , then
    Recreate E-file
    .
  7. When you attach the document, select
    User defined description
    .
  8. Enter
    8082 Signature Document
     for the description.
