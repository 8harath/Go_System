K-1-004 e-file error

Partner is missing City in the partner address.
You get this error when partner information is missing a city entry for a partner or partners.

Solution

If using direct K-1 import:
  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    Federal
    , then
    Direct K-1 Import
    .
  2. Select a partner,
    Import K1 Partner Information
    , then
    Item F
    . Make sure all partners have a full U.S. or foreign address entered.
  3. Do a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
If using K-1 aggregation:
  1. Review your aggregation file for any missing address information for each partner (U.S. or foreign).
  2. Re-upload the aggregation file.
  3. Do a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
