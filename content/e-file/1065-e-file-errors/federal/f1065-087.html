F1065-087 e-file error

Per IRS reject code F1065-087, At least 2 Federal Schedule K-1 must be included unless the Form 1065, Page 1, Amended is checked and Form 8082, Page 1, Part 1, BBA AAR is checked. Check the Suppress option location in the Organizer: Go to Organizer General Information Return and Print Options 3rd tab Sch K, K-1 and Activity Schedules Suppress has been selected for Schedule K-1 and partner activity schedules

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    General Information
    ,
    Return and Print Options
    ,
    Sch K, K-1 and Activity Schedules
    , then
    Print or Suppress
    .
  2. Mark the checkbox to print:
    • Schedule K-1, partner activity schedules, and Schedule K-3
    • Partner activity schedules
  3. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
