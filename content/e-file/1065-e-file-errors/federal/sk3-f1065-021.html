SK3-F1065-021 e-file error

If Schedule K-3 (Form 1065), PartIXAttached has a choice of "Yes" indicated, then SchK3PartnerInfoBEATGrp must have a value.
This happens when the e-file doesn't include partner or K-3 information.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    General Information
    , and then
    Return and Print Options
    .
  2. Select
    Sch K, K-1 and Activity Schedules
    .
  3. Make sure these items aren't suppressed. If they are, select
    Print
    .
    1. Schedule K-1, partner activity schedules, and Schedule K-3
    2. Partner activity schedules
  4. Next go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Partner Information
    ,
    Common Partner Data
    .
  5. Select
    Common K-3 Print Options
    .
  6. Select
    All partners' Schedule K-3
    .
  7. Perform a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
