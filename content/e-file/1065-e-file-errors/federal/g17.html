G17 e-file error

Error

The element is not recorded or does not contain a value (i.e. the value is null).

Cause

The name or address for the foreign institution is missing.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then
    Foreign Bank Account
    .
  2. Select
    FinCen
    , then
    Part V
    .
  3. Open the financial institute information and correct the name and address fields.
  4. Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit.
