E-file diagnostic: Form 5471, Page 1, Section H

Form 5471, Page 1, Section H: The Meeting Filing Requirement Statement must be completed for each person entered in Section H

Solution

  1. Go to the
    Organizer
     folder and select
    Foreign Information
    , then
    Foreign Corporation
    .
  2. Select the entity, then
    Filer Information
    .
  3. Select
    Person
    , then
    Person on Whose Behalf 5471 is Filed
    , then the name.
  4. On the
    Meeting Filing Requirements Statement
     tab, do 1 of the following:
    • Mark the
      Suppress Meeting Filing Requirements Statement box
       checkbox.
    • Enter
      Requirements met description
      ,
      Return Name
      ,
      Service Center
      , and an address.
  5. Do a full-recompute and re-create the e-file.
