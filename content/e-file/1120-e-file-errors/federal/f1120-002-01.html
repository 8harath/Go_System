F1120-002-01 e-file error 

The box is checked to indicate "Personal Holding Company" but Schedule PH is not attached.
This happens when the form indicates a Personal Holding Company but Schedule PH is not attached.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    , then
    Federal
    , and select
    1120-US Corporation Income Tax Return
     page 1.
  2. Ensure that the field for Line 2
    Personal Holding co. (attach Sch. PH)
     is checked.
  3. Go to
    Organizer
    , then
    Federal E-file
    .
  4. Select
    Additional Information
    , and check
    Forms Included in E-file
     to verify that the field for Schedule PH is checked to be included in the e-file.
  5. Go to
    Tax Forms
    , then
    Federal
    , and select
    Sch PH - Personal Holding Company Tax
     Page 1, Part III.
  6. Populate Line 26 on Schedule PH. You can enter
    NONE
     on this line.
  7. Do a full recompute and resubmit the e-file.
note
You may need to go to
Print
, select
Selected Federal F Corp
, scroll down to
Form 4626
, and select one of the following options:
Preview
,
Create to PDF
or
Send to Safesend
. Create a PDF and attach it to the return.
