FPYPMT-004 e-file error

IRS Payment Record: Rule Number: FPYPMT-004 Requested Payment Date in the IRS Payment Record must be on or before the due date of the return.
This happens when a return that is filed past the original due date has a payment date that is in the future.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    General Information
    , then
    Bank information
    .
  2. In the
    DIRECT DEBIT (ELECTRONIC FUNDS WITHDRAW)
    section, change the date to be the same date as the return will be submitted.
  3. Recreate and resubmit the e-file.
