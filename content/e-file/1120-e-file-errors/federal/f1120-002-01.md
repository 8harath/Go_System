# F1120-002-01 e-file error

```
The box is checked to indicate "Personal Holding Company" but Schedule PH is not attached.
```

This happens when the form indicates a Personal Holding Company but Schedule PH is not attached.

## Solution

- Go to **Tax Forms**, then **Federal**, and select **1120-US Corporation Income Tax Return** page 1.
- Ensure that the field for Line 2 **Personal Holding co. (attach Sch. PH)** is checked.
- Go to **Organizer**, then **Federal E-file**.
- Select **Additional Information**, and check **Forms Included in E-file** to verify that the field for Schedule PH is checked to be included in the e-file.
- Go to **Tax Forms**, then **Federal**, and select **Sch PH - Personal Holding Company Tax** Page 1, Part III.
- Populate Line 26 on Schedule PH. You can enter **NONE** on this line.
- Do a full recompute and resubmit the e-file.

note

You may need to go to **Print**, select **Selected Federal F Corp**, scroll down to **Form 4626**, and select one of the following options: **Preview**, **Create to PDF** or **Send to Safesend**. Create a PDF and attach it to the return.
