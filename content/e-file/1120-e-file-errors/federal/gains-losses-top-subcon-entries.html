E-file diagnostic: Gains and losses detail entries in top con and subcon returns not supported

Gains and Losses (1120 Schedule D and/or Form 4797): Detail entries in top con and subcon returns is not supported and will cause print, e-file, and compute issues. All detail must be entered in the member company returns. Navigate to "Organizer \ Gains and Losses \ Compute and Print Options \ Deactivation" and follow the on-screen instructions to remove the unsupported data entry in this return.
This happens when dispositions have been entered on a consolidated return instead of on the parent or subsidiary returns.

Solution

You meed to enter any dispositions on a consolidated return in the parent and or subsidiary returns.
  1. In Organizer, select
    Gains and Losses
    , then
    Compute and Print Options
    .
  2. Select
    Deactivation
    .
  3. Read the notes carefully and make a note of all the information to delete.
  4. Select
    1) Press this button to delete all gains information
    .
  5. Enter any disposition in the parent and or subsidiary returns.
  6. In the topcon open Organizer, select
    Consolidated Returns
    .
  7. Select
    Step 5 - Consolidate
    .
  8. Select
    Consolidate
    .
