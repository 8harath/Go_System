F1120-112-02 e-file error

Form 1120: Rule Number: F1120-112-02 Attachment of Form 2220 is indicated on page 1 but Form 2220 is not attached.
This happens when penalty has no value.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    , then
    Federal
     and select
    2220 - Underpayment of Estimated Tax
    .
  2. Go to
    Underpayment of Estimated Tax
    ,
    Page 2
    .
  3. Enter a value or
    NONE
     in
    Line 38
    .
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file
