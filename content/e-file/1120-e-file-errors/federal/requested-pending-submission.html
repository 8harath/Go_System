Requested - pending submission.
This happens due to the IRS being shutdown to update their system for the upcoming tax year. This applies to both federal and state e-files.

Solution

All e-file submissions received by Thomson Reuters during the IRS shutdown will show a status of pending with a timestamp of when they were originally processed to submit.
important
This annual shutdown doesn't extend the IRS 10-day transmission perfection period.
