E-file error: BooksAddress cannot be empty

BooksAddress cannot be empty
An address field for Books and Records is empty.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , then
    Common State
    .
  2. Select
    General Information
    ,
    Books and Records
    ,
    Location of Books and Records
    .
  3. Enter the complete domestic or foreign address.
  4. Make sure that Columns C, E, F, G, and H have an entry.
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
