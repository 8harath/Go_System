000032 e-file error

000032: Tax Year must be equal the Year portion of Tax Period Begin Date CCYY or Tax Period End Date CCYY.
This happens when the fiscal year return isn’t in the current year.
Solution
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then to the
    General Information
     folder.
  2. Go to
    Basic Return Information
    .
  3. Go to the
    Entity Information
     tab.
  4. Go to the
    DATES
     section.
  5. The
    Fiscal year beginning
     and
    Fiscal year ending
     need to be in the current year.
  6. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
