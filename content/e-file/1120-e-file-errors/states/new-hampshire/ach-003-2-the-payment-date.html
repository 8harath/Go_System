E-file diagnostic: ACH-003-2

ACH-003-2: The Payment Date cannot be greater than or less than the Electronic Postmark Date. (Automatic GO TO not available) Please go to Organizer / States / Common State / General Information / Bank Information / Payment of Estimates tab / and update the payment date for Estimate 3.
This happens when the payment date is in the future and needs to be before or equal to the filing date.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    .
  2. Go to
    Common state
    General Information
    Bank Information
    .
  3. Go to the
    Payment of Estimates
     tab.
  4. Update the date for the estimate payment you are making with today’s date.
    note
    The date should be in Central Standard time (CT).
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
