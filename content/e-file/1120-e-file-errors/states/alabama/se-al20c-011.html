SE-AL20C-011 e-file error

Error

20C Pg 1 Part of consol federal and Sch E line 12 not equal to 0 or NONE. Attach: 1552a1.pdf.

Cause

You receive this error when Alabama Form 20C, Schedule E, line 12 is greater than 0, and there's no 1552a1.pdf attached to the e-file.

Solution

Review Alabama's Form 20C, page 4, Schedule E, line 12.
If Schedule E, line 12 is greater than 0:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Alabama
    ,
    E-file
    ,
    Attachments
    , and then
    Summary
    .
  2. Select
    Attach PDF
    .
  3. Select
    Add
    .
  4. Select Alabama for
    Attachment Jurisdiction
    .
  5. Go to the file to upload.
  6. Select 1552a1 for
    Predefined description
    .
  7. Select
    Upload
    , and then
    Done
    .
  8. Perform a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
If Schedule E, line 12 is blank:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Alabama
    ,
    E-file
    ,
    Attachments
    , and then
    Summary
    .
  2. Remove any 1552a1.pdf attachment, if any.
  3. Perform a full re-compute, re-create the e-file, then re-submit.
