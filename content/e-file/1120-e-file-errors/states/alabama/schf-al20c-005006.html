SchF-AL20C-005/006 e-file error

If Schedule F, line 9, BOY and/or EOY is populated then a pdf named OtherInvestments.pdf or WksNetWorthStmts.pdf is required to be attached. Note: Do not include the .pdf in the actual pdf name. The .pdf extension is automatically added when e-file is created.
This happens when Alabama Form 20C, Page 4, Schedule F, Line 9 is populated on either Beginning of tax year column or End of tax year column, and there's no attached PDF file related to the Line 9 entry.
Solution
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Alabama
    , and
    E-file
    .
  2. Select
    Attachment
    , then
    Summary
    .
  3. Select the
    Attach PDF
     button, and attach the PDF attachments following the same file name and description of:
    OtherInvestments.pdf
     or
    WksNetWorthStmts.pdf
    .
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
