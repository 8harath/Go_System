# AL20C-001a e-file error

```
AL20C-001a: if fiscal year checkbox is populated and the 52/53 week filer checkbox is NOT populated, then Tax period Begin Date should be greater than or equal to 02/01/XXXX and less than or equal to 12/31/XXXX.
```

This happens when fiscal year dates fields have values, this implies a fiscal year return.

**Solution**

- Go to **Tax form**, **States**, and select **Alabama**.
- Go to **Alabama Privilege tax**, **CPT-Privilege Tax Return**, and then **CPT Page 1**.
- Verify that the line **1a** has an **X**.
- If this field is marked, remove the dates in the organizer.
- Go to **Organizer**, **General Information**, and **Basic Return information**.
- In the **Dates** section, remove dates in the **Fiscal year beginning** and **Fiscal year ending** fields as having dates in those fields would imply it's a fiscal year return.
