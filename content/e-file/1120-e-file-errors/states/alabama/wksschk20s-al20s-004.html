WksSchK20s-AL20S-004 e-file error 

WksSchK20s-AL20S-004: Total other investment items detail should equal the amount reported on the Form 20S, Schedule K, Line 20c, Apportioned Amount. Drill down on Schedule K, Line 20c to enter detail.
This happens when the amounts don’t equal as the detail from Federal doesn’t apply to Alabama.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Alabama
    , then
    Reconciliations
    .
  2. Go to
    Federal 1120S Schedule K amounts as adjusted for Alabama income tax law section
    .
  3. Go to the
    Other Investment Items and Amounts Description
     Grid.
  4. Enter the
    Description
     and
    AL Apportioned Amt
     amounts.
    note
    Ensure the total of the amounts entered here match with what is on Form 20S, Page 4, Line 20c.
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
