FORM500-01181-011 e-file error

FORM500-01181-011: Navigation : Tax forms / Maryland / Form500C / Page 2 / line 3d.
This happens when in Form 500 Page 2 in line 3d is not equal to Form 500DM Page 1 line 6.

Solution

  1. In Tax Forms, select
    States
    , then
    Maryland
    .
  2. Select
    Form 500-Corporation Income Tax Return, Page 2
    , line 3(d).
  3. In Tax Forms, in
    Maryland
     the value on line 3(d) must be equal to the amount on Form 500DM, Page 1, line 6 .
  4. Perform a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
