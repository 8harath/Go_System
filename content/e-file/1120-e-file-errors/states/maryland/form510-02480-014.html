FORM510-02480-014 e-file error

FORM510-02480-014: Schedule B Individual Members - Distributive share of tax paid must be provided. If 0, enter 0.
This happens when Schedule B Individual Members - Distributive share of tax paid is missing.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    , then the
    Maryland
     folder.
  2. Go to
    510-PTE Income Tax Return
    , and select
    Shareholder Info
    .
  3. Select the
    Shareholder
     name. The
    Nonresident distr. or pro rata of tax paid
     line needs to have a value.
  4. To enter the amount, go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , then the
    Maryland
     folder.
  5. Select
    S-Corporation Items only
    , then go to the
    S Corporation Only Items
     section.
  6. Enter the
    Amount
     or enter
    None
     in
    Nonresident shareholders distributive or pro rata share (Override)
     line.
  7. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
