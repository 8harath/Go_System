# FORM510-02480-014 e-file error

```
FORM510-02480-014: Schedule B Individual Members - Distributive share of tax paid must be provided. If 0, enter 0.
```

This happens when Schedule B Individual Members - Distributive share of tax paid is missing.

## Solution

- Go to **Tax Forms**, **States**, then the **Maryland** folder.
- Go to **510-PTE Income Tax Return**, and select **Shareholder Info**.
- Select the **Shareholder** name. The **Nonresident distr. or pro rata of tax paid** line needs to have a value.
- To enter the amount, go to **Organizer**, **States**, then the **Maryland** folder.
- Select **S-Corporation Items only**, then go to the **S Corporation Only Items** section.
- Enter the **Amount** or enter **None** in **Nonresident shareholders distributive or pro rata share (Override)** line.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
