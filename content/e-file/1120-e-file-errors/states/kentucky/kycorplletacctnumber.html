KyCorpLLETAcctNumber Validation e-file error

Form: SchLLET[1] KyCorpLLETAcctNumber[1]
Description: Kentucky Corporation Account Number/LLET Account Number
LineNumber: 
Requirement: KY Corporate LLET Account Number 6 Digits
Error: The field 'KyCorpLLETAcctNumber' with value '0', data is missing.
This happens when the account number is missing. A 6-digit account number is required.

Solution

  1. Select
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Common State
    ,
    General Information
    ,
    State
    , and
    City ID Numbers
    .
  2. Go to Kentucky, and enter the account number. The account number has to be 6 digits.
An option to omit the account number doesn't exist. Contact the Department of revenue for Kentucky if an LLC account number isn't available.
