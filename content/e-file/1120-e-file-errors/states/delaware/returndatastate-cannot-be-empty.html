Error: ReturnDataState Cannot Be Empty

This e-file validation error happens when the Delaware extension doesn't have an estimated income amount. The
ReturnDataState
 field needs a value before you can create the e-file.

Error message

Validation failed on CXXXXXX5.ide: Error: ReturnDataState ActionNeeded: Field 'ReturnDataState' cannot be empty Element Name: ReturnDataState XPath: /ReturnState\[1]/ReturnDataState\[1] XML Fragment: Field Key: 0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0 Error Code: c00ce011 Error Reason: Element '{http://www.irs.gov/efile}ReturnDataState' cannot be empty according to the DTD/Schema

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Delaware
    General Information
    .
  2. In the
    EXTENSION | ESTIMATE
     section, go to
    Estimated amount of distributive (S corp) or taxable (C corp) income for the taxable year
    .
  3. Enter
    NONE
     if there's no income, or enter the estimated amount.
  4. Recreate the e-file to clear the validation error.
