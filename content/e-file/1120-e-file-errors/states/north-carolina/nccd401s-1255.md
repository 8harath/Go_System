# NCCD401S-1255 e-file error

```
Schedule B Line 19 must equal the sum of Lines 16, 17 and 18 multiplied by 0.0475 (with the result rounded to the nearest whole number), if the sum of Lines 16, 17, and 18 are positive.
```

The system expects to have a value in North Carolina Form CD-401S, Page 2 Line 19. It should be the sum of Lines 16, 17 and 18 multiplied by 0.0475 (with the result rounded off to the nearest whole number).

## Solution

To populate the correct amount:

- In Organizer, select **States**, then**North Carolina**.
- Select **Tax Computation**.
- Look for the **Schedule B- Nonresident shareholders ONLY** section.
- Enter the amount in the **Total tax (Override)** field.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
