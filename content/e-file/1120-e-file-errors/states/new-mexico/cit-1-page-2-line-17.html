E-file diagnostic: If CIT-1 page 2 line 17 has an amount, form RPD-41379 must be attached

Form CIT-1: If CIT-1 page 2 line 17 has an amount, form RPD-41379 must be attached.
This happens when the New Mexico form CIT-1 Page 2 Line 17 has an amount but the RPD-41379 PDF is missing.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , then the
    New Mexico
     folder.
  2. Go to
    E-file
    , and the
    Forms Included in E-file
     folder.
  3. Select the
    RPD-41379
     field.
  4. Attach your RPD-41379 PDF document to New Mexico.
  5. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , and the
    New Mexico
     folder.
  6. Go to
    E-file
    ,
    Attachments
    , and the
    Summary
     folder.
  7. Select
    Attach PDF
    .
  8. In the
    Electronic Filing Attachments
     screen, select
    Add
    .
  9. For the
    Jurisdiction
    , select
    New Mexico
    .
  10. Find your RPD-41379.pdf and attach it.
  11. Select the
    User Defined Description
    , and make sure the name matches the PDF of RPD-41379.pdf.
  12. Select
    Upload
    .
  13. Do a full recompute, and recreate the e-file.
