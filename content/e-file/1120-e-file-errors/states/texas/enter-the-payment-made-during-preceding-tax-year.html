E-file diagnostic: Enter the payment made during the preceding tax year

Texas prior year tax payment: Enter the payment made during the preceding tax year.
This happens when you still need to report the amount of taxes paid in the previous tax year.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    State Franchise Tax
    Texas Franchise
     folder.
  2. Select the
    General Information
     folder, then
    General Information
     tab.
  3. In the
    Payment of Tax During Preceding Tax year
     section, select another option besides
    Blank
    .
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
