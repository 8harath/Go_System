# E-file diagnostic: Enter the payment made during the preceding tax year

```
Texas prior year tax payment: Enter the payment made during the preceding tax year.
```

This happens when you still need to report the amount of taxes paid in the previous tax year.

## Solution

- Go to 

  **Organizer****State Franchise Tax****Texas Franchise**

   folder.
- Select the **General Information** folder, then **General Information** tab.
- In the **Payment of Tax During Preceding Tax year** section, select another option besides **Blank**.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
