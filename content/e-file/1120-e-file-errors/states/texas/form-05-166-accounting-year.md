# Form 05-166 accounting year begin and end dates e-file error

```
Form 05-166: Accounting year begin and end dates for each affiliate must be within the accounting year begin and end dates of the primary report. (Automatic GOTO is not available.)
```

## Solution

- Go to **Organizer**, **State Franchise Tax**, **Texas Franchise**, **Combined Return Information**, and then **Reporting Entity**.
- Review the entries for accounting year begin and end date. These must be the same as the Form 05-158, which you can find in **Tax Forms**, **States**, **Texas, 05-158**, **Franchise Tax Report**, **05-158A, Franchise Tax Report, P1**, **Dates**.
- Perform a full recompute, recreate the e-file, and submit.
