Texas return invalid security information

Texas return invalid security information: Webfile number or last reported revenue is blank.
This happens when the webfile number or last reported revenue is blank.

Solution

For 1120 returns:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    State Franchise Tax
    ,
    Texas Franchise
    , and then
    General Information
    .
  2. Select
    Paid Preparer Information
     tab, then go to
    Preparer Validation
     section.
  3. Enter the
    Webfile number
    . The webfile number is mailed to all franchise taxpayers. For questions, contact the state at 800-442-3453.
  4. On the same screen and section, verify that your
    Total revenue on latest return
     is populated with a positive amount or NONE.
  5. Perform a full recompute, recreate the e-file, and resubmit.
For 1065 returns:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Texas
    ,
  2. Select
    General Information
    , then go to
    Paid Preparer Information
     tab.
  3. Go to
    Preparer Validation
     section, enter
    Webfile number
    .
  4. On the same screen and section, verify that your
    Total revenue on latest return
     is populated with a positive amount or NONE.
  5. Do a full Recompute and Recreate the E-file
note
The Webfile number is mailed to all State of Texas Franchise taxpayers, or you may reach out to the state at 1-800-442-3453 for webfile number.
