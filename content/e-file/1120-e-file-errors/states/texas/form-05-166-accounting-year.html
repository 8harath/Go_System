Form 05-166 accounting year begin and end dates e-file error

Form 05-166: Accounting year begin and end dates for each affiliate must be within the accounting year begin and end dates of the primary report. (Automatic GOTO is not available.)

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    State Franchise Tax
    ,
    Texas Franchise
    ,
    Combined Return Information
    , and then
    Reporting Entity
    .
  2. Review the entries for accounting year begin and end date. These must be the same as the Form 05-158, which you can find in
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    Texas, 05-158
    ,
    Franchise Tax Report
    ,
    05-158A, Franchise Tax Report, P1
    ,
    Dates
    .
  3. Perform a full recompute, recreate the e-file, and submit.
