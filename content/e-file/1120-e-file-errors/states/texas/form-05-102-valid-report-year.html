Form 05-102: Valid report year is 1992 through 2023.

Form 05-102: Valid report year is 1992 through 2023. Review your input.
This happens when the report year isn't somewhere between 1992-2023.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    Texas
    ,
    05-158-A, Franchise Tax Report
    , and then
    05-158A, Franchise Tax Report, P1
    .
  2. Review the account year end date. It can't be after 1/4 of the report year. To correct the report year, go to
    Organizer
    ,
    State Franchise Tax
    ,
    Texas Franchise
    , and then
    General Information
    .
    note
    The report year pertains to the year the tax return was filed in. For example, if the return's being filed on 11/15/2023, then the report year would be 2023.
  3. Perform a full re-compute and re-create the e-file.
