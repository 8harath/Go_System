# 5205 e-file error

```
Invalid combination of tax forms.
```

This happens when a **No Tax Due** scenario occurs and only Form 05-102 and Form 05-166 are generating within the e-file.

## Solution 1

Enable the e-file option for form 05-102:

- Go to 

  **Organizer****State Franchise Tax****Texas Franchise****E-file**

   folder.
- Select **Additional Information**, then go to **E-file Form 05-102 Option** section.
- Mark the **Opt** checkbox to e-file Form 05-102 only.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.

## Solution 2

If you don’t have a No Tax Due return:

- Go to 

  **Organizer****State Franchise Tax****Texas Franchise**

   folder.
- Select **Return Options**, then go to the **No Tax Due Report** section.
- Make sure both of these lines are blank:


  - **Annualized total revenue is below the no tax due threshold**.
  - **This entity has zero Texas gross receipts**.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
