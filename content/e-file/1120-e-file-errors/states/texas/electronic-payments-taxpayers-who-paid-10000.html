E-file diagnostic: Electronic Payments: Taxpayers who paid $10,000

Electronic Payments: Taxpayers who paid $10,000 or more during the preceding fiscal year are required to electronically pay their franchise tax. For more information, visit http://www.window.state.tx.
This happens when the Texas 2nd extension amount is not $10,000 or greater.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Texas
    General Information
    .
  2. Select the
    General Information
     tab, then
    Payment of Tax During Preceding Tax Year (Mandatory)
     section.
  3. Make a selection other than
    Blank
     or less than
    $10,000
    .
