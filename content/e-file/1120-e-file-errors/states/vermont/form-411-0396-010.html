E-file diagnostic: Form 411-0396-010

Form 411-0396-010: VT Apportionment percentage is less than 100 percent Form BA402 must be attached.
This happens when the BA402 isn't included in the e-file but is required.

Solution 1

  1. Select
    Organizer
    , then the
    States
     folder.
  2. Select
    Vermont
    , then the
    General Information
     folder.
  3. Go to the
    General Information
     section.
  4. Mark the
    Corporation has nexus in Vermont
     checkbox.
  5. Do a full recompute then recreate the e-file.

Solution 2

  1. Select
    Organizer
    , then the
    States
     folder.
  2. Select
    Vermont
    ,
    E-file
    , then the
    Forms Included in E-file
     folder.
  3. Mark the
    BA402
     checkbox.
  4. Do a full recompute then recreate the e-file.
note
If this is a consolidated return, at least 1 subsidiary must be marked with nexus in Vermont.
