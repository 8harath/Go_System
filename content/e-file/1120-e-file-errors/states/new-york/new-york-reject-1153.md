# E-file diagnostic: New York Reject 1153

```
New York Reject 1153: The state of New York will reject any e-filed extension with liability period of less than one month or greater than one year. Extensions with a liability period of less than one month must be paper filed.
```

This happens when an extension with the liability period of less than 1 month or greater than 1 year and Extensions with a liability period of less than 1 month.

## Solution

- Go to 

  **Organizer****Basic Return Information****Entity Information****Dates**

   section.
- Make sure the **Fiscal year beginning** and **Fiscal year ending** dates don’t equal a period of less than 1 month for New York E-filing purposes, or greater than 1 year in total.

note

The state of New York will reject any e-filed return with a liability period of less than 1 month or greater than 1 year. Returns with a liability period of less than 1 month must be paper filed.
