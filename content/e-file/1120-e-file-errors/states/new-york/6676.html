6676 e-file error

Form must be present when required attachments checkbox is checked.
This happens when there are missing attachments.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    ,
    States
    ,
    New York
    ,
    CT-3
    , then
    Page 2
    .
  2. In
    Section C
    , line
    3
    , if
    CT-225
     is marked, then an attachment is also needed.
  3. To attach PDF, go to
    Organizer
     folder:
    States
     then
    New York
    .
  4. Select
    E-file
    ,
    Attachments
    , and
    Summary
    . Attach your PDF and select the option
    for Form CT-225-A/B
    in the
    PDF Attachments Included in E-File
     section.
To Attach PDF:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    New York
    .
  2. E-file
    ,
    Attachments
    ,
    Summary
    .
  3. Select
    Attach PDF
    . This opens the
    Electronic Filing Attachments Dialogue
    .
  4. Select
    Add
    , then select the
    Attachment Jurisdiction
    : choose
    New York
    .
  5. Select
    Browse for file
     and add your attachment.
  6. Use a
    Predefined description
    , and select
    UPLOAD
    .
  7. Do a full recompute, and recreate the e-file.
note
On the CT-3-page 2, Section C, don't override any of these boxes or your return will be rejected.
