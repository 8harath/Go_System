E-file diagnostic: Form CT-225-A/B should be attached as a PDF

Electronic Filing: Form CT-225-A/B should be attached as a PDF. Check the box indicating it has been attached to clear this diagnostic.
This happens when a PDF was attached but not included in the XML.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    New York
    .
  2. Continue to the
    E-file
    Attachments
    Summary
    .
  3. Find the
    PDF Attachments Included in E-File
     section and mark the field for
    Forms CT-225-A/B
     and
    CT-399
    .
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
