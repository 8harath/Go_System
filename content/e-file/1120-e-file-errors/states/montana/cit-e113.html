CIT-E113 e-file error

Error

Missing supporting schedule for page 3, Line 3g. (CIT-E113)

Cause

The Other Reductions Schedule isn't attached to the return.

Solution

  1. In the
    Tax Forms
    , go to
    States
    .
  2. Select
    Montana
    , then
    CIT - Corporation Income Tax Return
    .
  3. Check Form CIT, Page 3, line 3g. If it's correct, you need to include a detailed breakdown attachment as a PDF.
    1. Go to the
      Organizer
      ,
      States
      , then
      Montana
      .
    2. Go to
      E-file
      , then
      Attachments
      .
    3. In the Summary section, add an attachment with the name "OtherReductionsSchedule.pdf".
    4. Do a full re-compute, recreate the e-file and resubmit the return.
