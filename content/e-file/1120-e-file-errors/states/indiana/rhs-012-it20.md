# RHS-012-IT20 e-file error

```
RHS-012-IT20: PreparerUSAddress.AddressLine1Txt cannot exceed 30 characters.
```

This happens when Indiana Paid Preparer’s address exceeds 30 characters and may have special characters and/or spaces.

## Solution

- Go to 

  **Organizer****States**

  .
- Select **Paid Preparer Information**, then **Firm Information**.
- In the Indiana row (18), make sure the entry in the **Address** field for Column F or (foreign or domestic, Column I) is not more than 30 characters (with no spaces or special characters).
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
