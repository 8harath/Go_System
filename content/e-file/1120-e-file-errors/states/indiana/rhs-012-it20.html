RHS-012-IT20 e-file error

RHS-012-IT20: PreparerUSAddress.AddressLine1Txt cannot exceed 30 characters.
This happens when Indiana Paid Preparer’s address exceeds 30 characters and may have special characters and/or spaces.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    .
  2. Select
    Paid Preparer Information
    , then
    Firm Information
    .
  3. In the Indiana row (18), make sure the entry in the
    Address
     field for Column F or (foreign or domestic, Column I) is not more than 30 characters (with no spaces or special characters).
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
