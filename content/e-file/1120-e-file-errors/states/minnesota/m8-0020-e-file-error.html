M8-0020 e-file error

1120 Minnesota * M8-0020: Business Representative email must meet email address standards
This happens when the business representative e-mail is incomplete.

Solution

  1. In the Organizer, select
    States
    , then
    Common State
    .
  2. Select
    General Information
    , then
    Basic Return Information
    .
  3. Select the
    Signature Information
     tab.
  4. Go to the line for
    Minnesota
    ,
    Column G
    .
  5. Enter the correct e-mail address.
  6. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
