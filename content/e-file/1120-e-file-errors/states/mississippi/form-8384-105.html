Solution
  1. Go to the
    Organizer
    ,
    Income and deductions
     folder, then
    deductions
    .
  2. Go to
    Officers Compensation
    ,
    Individual Officers
    , then
    Name
    .
  3. Select the
    States
     hyperlink and select
    Mississippi
    .
  4. Enter the details. Include Name, Title, Address, SSN, and ownership percentage.
  5. Do a full recompute.
  6. Recreate the e-file.
