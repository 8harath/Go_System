2055 e-file error

If Line E equals Standard then Schedule E Lines E4 and E5 is required. (2055)
This error happens when the STANDARD box on line E of Arizona Form 120 is marked but there are no standard apportionment values for lines E4 and E5 of Schedule E.

Solution

  1. Review lines E4 and E5 of Schedule E to ensure the standard apportionment values are valid. Invalid values are NONE, 0 or blank.
  2. Make the necessary changes in the application.
  3. If there are no apportionment values to report, select
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Arizona
    ,
    Allocation and Apportionment
    .
  4. In the
    Multi-State Apportionment
     section, select
    Not Applicable
     or
    Sales Factor Only
    .
  5. Recompute, recreate then retransmit the return.
