2015: 120 e-file error

* 2015: 120: If Line E contains a value for Air Carrier, Standard, or Sales Factor Only, Lines 6, 8, 9, 10 and 12 cannot be NULL.
This happens when the apportionment is not populated for the state, or it is none.

Solution

  1. In Organizer, select
    States
    , then
    Arizona
    .
  2. Select
    Allocation and Apportionment
    .
  3. Go to the
    Multi-state Apportionment
     section.
  4. Select the option
    Not Applicable
    .
  5. In the
    Overrides
     section, go to the
    Other income allocation to Arizona
     line and enter in
    None
    .
  6. Run a full recompute, then recreate the e-file.
