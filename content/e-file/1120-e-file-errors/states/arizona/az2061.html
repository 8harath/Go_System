AZ2061 e-file error

If Line 14 contains a value larger than zero, attach a Line 14 computation
schedule PDF titled 'NOLSch'. (Rule Number: AZ2061)
This error happens when line 14 of Arizona Form 120 reports an amount greater than $0.00 but a separate net operating loss (NOL) computation schedule was not attached to the e-filed return.

Solution

  1. To attach a PDF, do one of the following:
    • Select
      Edit
      , then
      Insert e-file Attachments
      .
    • Select
      Organizer
      ,
      States
      ,
      Arizona
      E-File Attachments
      ,
      PDF Summary
      , then
      Attach PDF
      .
  2. Recompute, recreate then retransmit the return.
