F540NR-960 e-file error

Error

If Form 540NR Line 91 [SharedResponsibilityPenalty] is present, then Form 3853 must be attached.

Resolution

  1. In the
    Organizer
    , go to
    States
    .
  2. Select
    California
    , then
    General Information
    .
  3. Go to Health Care, Health Care, then select the
    Shared Responsibility Penalty
     tab.
  4. In the
    Full Year Coverage Declaration
     section, answer
    Yes
     or
    No
    .
  5. If you answered
    No
    , drill down on the name and fill out the information to calculate the penalty.
  6. Do a Full Recompute, recreate the electronic file, and resubmit the return.
