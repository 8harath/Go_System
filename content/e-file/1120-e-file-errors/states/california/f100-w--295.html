F100(W)-295 e-file error

Error

Rule Number F100(W)-295: If Form 100/100W, Line 33 [Withholding] is present, then either Form 592-B or Form 593 must be attached.

Cause

Form 100/100W, Line 33 [Withholding] is present, but Form 592-B or Form 593 is not attached.

Solution

  1. In the
    Organizer
    , go to
    States
    .
  2. Select
    California
    , then
    E-file
    .
  3. Add either Nonresident Withholding (Form 592-B) or Real Estate Withholding (Form 593-B) at the member level.
  4. Re-compute the member.
  5. Reconsolidate, then recreate the XML.
