F1120N-0001 e-file error

Rule F1120N-0001: If filing combined return, must include NE Schedule III and IV.
This happens when 1120N, Sch III doesn't have any data.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    States
    Nebraska
    .
  2. Select
    1120N - Corporate Income Tax Return
    , then go to
    Page 1120N Sch III
    .
  3. In
    Column D
     there must be an amount or
    NONE
    .
  4. Enter
    NONE
     for the
    Totals
     row if you don’t have an amount.
  5. Follow these steps to enter data.
    1. Go to the
      Organizer
      States
      Allocation and Apportionment
      .
    2. Go to
      Organizer / Overrides
      Nebraska
      .
    3. Select
      Sales
      , then go to the
      Organizer - Nebraska Sales Apportionment
       section.
    4. Enter the sales apportionment amounts or
      NONE
      .
  6. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
