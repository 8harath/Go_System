# S1-0008 e-file error

```
Rule S1-0008: If Form 1120N Schedule III is present, Column D total must equal Form 1120N, Schedule I, Line 14b
```

This happens when the form 1120N Schedule III has an amount but doesn’t match the 1120N, Schedule I, line 14b on each member.

## Solution

- Go to 

  **Tax Forms****States****Nebraska****1120N-Corporate Income Tax Return**

  .
- Go to the **1120N sch III** tab.
- Make note of the amount in **Column D**, **Amount of Nebraska Sales or Receipts**.
- Go to the **1120N sch A & I** tab.
- Go to the **Nebraska Schedule I** section.
- **Line 14** of the **Nebraska** column needs to match the amount from step 3.
- These also need to match the Members on **line 14** and the **Topcon 1120N sch III**, **Detail**, column **D** for each member.
- Do a full recompute, reconsolidate, and recreate the e-file.
