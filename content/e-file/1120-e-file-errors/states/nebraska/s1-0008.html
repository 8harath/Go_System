S1-0008 e-file error

Rule S1-0008: If Form 1120N Schedule III is present, Column D total must equal Form 1120N, Schedule I, Line 14b
This happens when the form 1120N Schedule III has an amount but doesn’t match the 1120N, Schedule I, line 14b on each member.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    States
    Nebraska
    1120N-Corporate Income Tax Return
    .
  2. Go to the
    1120N sch III
     tab.
  3. Make note of the amount in
    Column D
    ,
    Amount of Nebraska Sales or Receipts
    .
  4. Go to the
    1120N sch A & I
     tab.
  5. Go to the
    Nebraska Schedule I
     section.
  6. Line 14
     of the
    Nebraska
     column needs to match the amount from step 3.
  7. These also need to match the Members on
    line 14
     and the
    Topcon 1120N sch III
    ,
    Detail
    , column
    D
     for each member.
  8. Do a full recompute, reconsolidate, and recreate the e-file.
