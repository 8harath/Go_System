E-file diagnostic: Wisconsin requires that Form 6, part II, Line 2b foot horizontally

Electronic Filing: The State of Wisconsin requires that Form 6, Part II, Line 2b, State taxes accrued or paid foot horizontally. The amounts from the parent and subsidiaries on this return do not match the amount in the combined column. The amounts for the parents and subsidiaries should be adjusted to match the combined amount
This diagnostic appears when the amounts for state taxes accrued or paid on Form 6, Part II, Line 2b don't foot horizontally. The State of Wisconsin requires that the parent and subsidiary amounts match the combined column total.

Solution

Follow these steps to resolve this diagnostic:
  1. In the
    Tax Forms
     section, select
    States
    , then
    Wisconsin
    .
  2. Select
    Combined Report
    ,
    6 – Combined Corporation Return, 6, Page 5
    , and review the
    Line 2b Combined Totals
     column.
  3. Right-click on
    Line 2b Combined
     and select
    Subview
     to review the lower member totals that are inconsistent with the
    Combined column line 2b Taxes Amount
    .
  4. Go to the
    Organizer
     and select
    States
    , then
    Wisconsin
    .
  5. Select
    State Adjustments
    , then
    State Accrued or paid (override)
    . Enter amounts in the parent and/or subsidiaries to ensure the total equals the combined column amount for Line 2b.
  6. Reconsolidate the return and recreate the e-file.
