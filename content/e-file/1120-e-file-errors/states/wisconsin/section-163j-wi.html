E-file diagnostic: Limitation on Business Interest Expense under Section 163(j): Wisconsin

Limitation on Business Interest Expense Under Section 163(j): Wisconsin has not adopted the Internal Revenue Code (IRC) limitation on the deduction of net business interest expense under IRC sec. 163(j). The State of Wisconsin has informed us that adjustments for Form 4 should be made on Schedules 4V, line 10 (other) or 4W, line 14 (other). Adjustments for Form 5s should be made on Schedule 5K, line 1, column C and adjustments for Form 6 should be made on Part II, lines 2j or 4n. Form 6 adjustments must be made at the parent or subsidiary level and cannot be made directly on the topcon. Note that the federal amount now reflects CARES changes.
This diagnostic appears because Wisconsin has not adopted the Internal Revenue Code (IRC) limitation on deducting net business interest expense under IRC Section 163(j).

Solution

This is a severe diagnostic but will not prevent you from e-filing. Follow these steps to resolve it:
  1. Select
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    , then the
    State and City Activation/Consolidation
     folder.
  2. Select
    State and City Activation/Consolidation
    , then go to the
    States
     tab. Right-click on
    Entity Type (column E)
     and select
    Subview
     to view lower members.
  3. Verify you have the 1 Parent, 1 Elim and the remaining entities are subsidiaries. Change
    Entity Type
     if needed.
  4. If you changed the entity type from
    Single
     to
    Subsidiary
    , perform a full recompute.
  5. Go to the
    Top Consolidation
     locator and select
    Consolidate
    .
  6. Go to the
    Organizer
     and select
    Consolidated Returns
    .
    1. Select
      What to Consolidate
      ,
      Consolidated Return List
      , then
      Subsidiary Listing Complete
      .
    2. Go to the
      Organizer
      and select
      Consolidated Returns
      .
    3. Select
      Review Preconsol Checklist
      ,
      Out of balance and compute pending
      , then
      Compute all Subsidiaries now
      .
    4. Select
      Consolidate
      .
  7. Recreate the e-file.
note
You can't have a single entity in a Top Consolidation Locator.
