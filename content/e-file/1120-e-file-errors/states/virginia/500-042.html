500-042 e-file error

Form 500: Rule Number: 500-042 If the corporation is not a multistate, Income Tax on Line 9 must equal .06 x Line 7.
This happens when apportionment computation doesn’t match to the actual apportionment percentage.

Solution 1

  1. In Tax Forms, select
    States
    ,
    Virginia
     then
    500 - Virginia Corp. Income Tax Return
    .
  2. Select the
    500, Page 2
     tab.
  3. Line 8a needs data that ties out with Sch 500a Section B line 3j.
  4. Form 500A only populates on the form when:
    1. the Property factor is less than 100%
    2. the payroll factor is less than 100%
    3. the Sales factor is less than 100%.
note
Make sure line 8(b) is less the 100%.

Solution 2

  1. In Tax Forms, select
    States
    , then
    Virginia
    , then
    Apportionment Forms
    .
  2. Select
    500, Sch A - Multistate Corporation
    .
  3. Go to Section B, line 1, column C.
  4. If 100% is entered here, then the amount won’t transfer to Form 500 Page 2 line 8a from 500 Sch A Section B line 3j.
  5. Remove the 100% to clear the reject.
  6. Perform a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
