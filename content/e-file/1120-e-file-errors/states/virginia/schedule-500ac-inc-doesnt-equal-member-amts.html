E-file diagnostic: Schedule 500AC income doesn’t equal member amounts

Schedule 500AC: Total of Schedule 500AC Income subject to Virginia tax does not equal the total of all member amounts.
This happens when line 16 doesn't equal the sum of lines 14 and 15.

Solution

  1. In Tax Forms, select
    States
    , then
    Virginia.
  2. Select
    500AC - Schedule of Affiliated Corp
    . parent, then
    500AC, Page 1
    .
  3. Line 16 total of all members needs to match the top con 500 Page 2 line 8a.
    note
    If amounts are totaling correctly, delete previously transferred state data at the Topcon. In Organizer, select
    States
    ,
    State Combined Returns
    ,
    State Consolidation Steps
    , then
    Step 6
    . Delete previously transferred state data, Virginia. Then consolidate the return.
