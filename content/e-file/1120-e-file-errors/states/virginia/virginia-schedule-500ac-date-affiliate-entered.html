Virginia Schedule 500AC: Date affiliate entered the group is a required entry e-file error

Schedule 500AC: Date affiliate entered the group is a required entry for e-file. Please enter the data in each subsidiary.
This e-file error will show if the date for each affiliate hasn’t been entered or blank.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    Virginia
    , then select
    Combined Consolidated Information
    .
  2. Select the
    Member of Affiliated Group
     section.
  3. Enter the information for the line
    Date became part of affiliated group
    .
  4. Once everything has been entered, select
    Transfer to All Members
    .
  5. Do a full recompute and reconsolidate the
    Topcon Return
    .
