E-file diagnostic: Missing Paid Preparer Information

Missing Paid Preparer Information: Paid Preparer Information has not been suppressed. Go to the Paid Preparer Information spreadsheet and enter the missing information. All information is required unless suppressed.
This happens when information is missing for the Paid preparer.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Common State
    ,
    General Information
    , then
    Basic return information
    .
  2. Select the
    Signature Authorization
     tab.
  3. On the
    Michigan
     row, make sure to fill in the following information:
    First name
    ,
    Last name
    ,
    Title
    ,
    E-Mail
    ,
    Phone
    , and the
    Signature date
    .
  4. Select
    Compute
    , and
    Full Recompute
    .
  5. Go to
    States
    ,
    State E-file
    , then
    Enable/create returns
    .
  6. Select the
    Create
     button for Michigan.
