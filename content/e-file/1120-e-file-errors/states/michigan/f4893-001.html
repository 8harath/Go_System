F4893-001 e-file error

If Form 4893 is present in the return, Form 4894 must also be present.
This happens when the 4893 is being included in the e-file but the 4894 isn't being included. If 4893 line 2 has an amount on it, then the 4894 needs to be included.

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    , then the
    States
     folder.
  2. Go to
    Michigan
    , then the
    Shareholder Information
     folder.
  3. Select
    Add new Shareholder and Officer Information
    .
  4. Fill out the information for this folder.
  5. Do a full recompute, and recreate the e-file.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Tax Forms
    , then the
    States
     folder.
  2. Go to
    Michigan CIT
    ,
    4893, CIT Small Business Alt. Credit
     folder.
  3. Go to line 2. If there is an amount on the line, then you will have to fill out the 4894 or remove the amount.
  4. If you don't want the amount on line 2, you can space through the amount.
  5. Do a full recompute, and recreate the e-file.
