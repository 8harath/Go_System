F4891-0032 e-file error

Business Rule F4891-003: If there is a value on Form 4891, line 34, then Form 4898 must be present in the MI xml file. Navigate to Organizer / States / Michigan / CIT Schedules / Non-Unitary Relations
This happens when an amount is present on Form 4891, Page 2, Line 34 but Form 4898 hasn’t been filled out.
Solution
  1. Go to the folder
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    Michigan
    .
  2. Select
    CIT Schedules
    ,
    Non-Unitary Relationship with FT entity...
    .
  3. Select
    Add new Non-Unitary Relationship with...
    .
  4. In the new window that appears, enter the name of the Entity and the required information.
  5. Select
    Compute
    , and
    Full Recompute
    .
