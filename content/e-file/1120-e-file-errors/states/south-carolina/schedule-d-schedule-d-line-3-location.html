E-file diagnostic: Schedule D: Schedule D line 3, Location 

Schedule D: Schedule D line 3, Location of the registered officer, city of the registered office, and Name of registered agent where registered office is located must be enter. If filing combined return, enter the Registered Agent In South Carolina information in the Parent return under Navigation: organizer\South Carolina\Annual Report\Registered Agent In South Carolina
This happens when the instruction is stating that on Schedule D, line 3, you need to provide: location of the registered office, city of the registered office, and name of the registered agent where the registered office is located.

Solution 1

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    South Carolina
     folder.
  2. In the
    Registered Agent in South Carolina
     section, select
    Annual Report
    .
  3. Enter
    Name
    ,
    Address
     and
    City
    .
  4. Do a full recompute.
  5. Recreate the e-file.
note
If you can't enter the previous information, you'll need to file the return on paper.

Solution 2

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    South Carolina
    ,
    Annual Report
     folder.
  2. Select
    Principal Business
    , then
    Nature of
    .
  3. Enter the information.
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
