E-file diagnostic: Schedule J: Schedule J line 4, Principal officer 

Schedule J: Schedule J line 4, Principal officer address and Nature of principal business in South Carolina information must be enter. If filing combined return, enter the above information in the Parent and Subsidiaries return under Navigation: Organizer\States\Common State\General Information\Books and Records\Principal Place of Business and Navigation: Organizer\South Carolina\Annual Report\Principal Business\Nature ofCarolina\Annual Report\ Principal Business\Nature of.
This happens when you are missing Nature of the business information required by the state.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    ,
    South Carolina
     folder.
  2. Select
    Annual Report
    .
  3. Go to the
    Principal Business
     section.
  4. Enter the details here for
    Line Nature Of
     for all the member locators.
  5. Do a full recompute for the members.
  6. Consolidate the Topcon.
  7. Recreate the e-file.
