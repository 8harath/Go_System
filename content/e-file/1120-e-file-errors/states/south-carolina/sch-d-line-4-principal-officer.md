# E-file Diagnostic: Schedule D Line 4, Principal officer address and Nature of principal business

```
Schedule D: Schedule D line 4, Principal officer address and Nature of principal business in South Carolina information must be enter.
```

This happens when you are missing information required by the state for the principal office address and Nature of principal businesses.

## Solution

- Go to the **Organizer**, **States**, **South Carolina** folder.
- Select **Annual Report**.
- In the **Principal Business** section, enter **Nature of**
- Do a Full Recompute.

note

If this is a consolidation, you will want to complete this information in the parent and subsidiaries.
