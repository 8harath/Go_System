# ILSCHUB-14750-1 e-file error

```
Schedule UB (Rule ILSCHUB-14750-1): Sch UB Step 2 Line 30 Col E must equal sum of all occurrences of Sch UB Step 2 Line 30 minus Sch UB Step 3 Line 1 Col D.
```

This happens when Form IL, Schedule UB Step 2 Line 30 Col E doesn't equal the sum of all occurrences of Sch UB Step 2 Line 30 minus Sch UB Step 3 Line 1 Col D.

## Solution

- Go to 

  **Tax Forms****States****Illinois****Combined Report****Sch UB-Combined Apportionment for UBG****Sch UB, Combined Apportionment****Sch UB Page 2 – Step 2**

   tab. Line 30 Column E must equal columns A through D combined.
- Go to 

  **Organizer****States****States Combined Returns****State Consolidation Steps****Step 6**

  . Select **Delete previously transferred state data** and follow the steps to clear previously transferred data for Illinois. This step ensures that only current subsidiary data is populating Schedule UB.
- Once completed, go to 

  **Organizer****Consolidated Returns****Step 4 – Out of balance and compute pending**

  . Select **Compute subsidiaries with compute pending** and **Compute all subsidiaries now**.
- Go to **Step 5 – Consolidate!** and select **Consolidate**.
- Recreate the e-file.

note

If your return contains SubCons or DivCons:



- Go to 

  **Tax Forms****States****Illinois****Combined Report****Sch UB-Combined Apportionment for UBG****Sch UB, Combined Apportionment****Sch UB Page 2 – Step 2**

   tab.
- Select **Important for Sub consolidated Returns** and follow the instructions in the Workpaper.
