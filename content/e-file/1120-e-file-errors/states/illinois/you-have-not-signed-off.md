# E-file diagnostic: Illinois Authorization: You have not signed off

```
Illinois Authorization: You have not signed off the perjury statement. Form IL1120 and its related schedules cannot be e-filed.
```

This happens when you haven’t selected an option for authorization to e-file the return.

## Solution

- Select **Organizer**, then **States**.
- Go to 

  **Illinois****E-file****Additional Information**

  .
- Go to the **AUTHORIZATION** section.
- Mark the **By Checking the box, I agree that I have examined the return, and to the best of my knowledge it is true, correct and complete** checkbox.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
