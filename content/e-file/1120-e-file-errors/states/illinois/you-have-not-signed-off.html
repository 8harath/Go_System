E-file diagnostic: Illinois Authorization: You have not signed off

Illinois Authorization: You have not signed off the perjury statement. Form IL1120 and its related schedules cannot be e-filed.
This happens when you haven’t selected an option for authorization to e-file the return.

Solution

  1. Select
    Organizer
    , then
    States
    .
  2. Go to
    Illinois
    E-file
    Additional Information
    .
  3. Go to the
    AUTHORIZATION
     section.
  4. Mark the
    By Checking the box, I agree that I have examined the return, and to the best of my knowledge it is true, correct and complete
     checkbox.
  5. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
