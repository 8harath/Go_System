ILK1P-200-1 e-file error

ILK1P-200-1: Either Sch K1P Step 1 Line 1 (partnership) or Sch K1P Step 1 Line 1 (S Corporation) must contain an X. Both cannot be marked.
This happens when the business type on the Sch K-1-P is missing.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    States
    .
  2. Go to
    Illinois
    ,
    State Adjustments
    .
  3. Go to
    Pass Through Input
    ,
    Schedule K-1-P
    .
  4. Select the
    Name
     of the pass-through.
  5. In the
    Step 1-Identify Your Partnership or S Corporation
     section, select either
    Partnership
     or
    S Corporation
     for the
    Business type
    .
  6. Run a full recompute, then recreate the e-file.
