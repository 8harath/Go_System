ILHeader-10700-1 e-file error

IL Return Header (Rule ILHeader-10700-1): IL-Header BusinesRepresentative/DateSigned cannot be blank.
This happens if the business representative column or data signed column is blank.

Solution

  1. Go to
    Organizer
    States
    .
  2. Select
    Common State
    , then
    General Information
  3. Select the
    Basic Return Information
     tab.
  4. Enter the
    signature date
     for
    IL
     in
    Column L
    .
  5. Go to the
    Estimates and Extensions
     section.
  6. Enter the date on both lines for
    Illinois
     in
    Extension Detail
    .
  7. Go to
    Paid Preparer Information
     in
    General Information
    .
  8. Select
    Illinois
     and enter the
    Signature Date
     in
    Column H
    .
  9. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
