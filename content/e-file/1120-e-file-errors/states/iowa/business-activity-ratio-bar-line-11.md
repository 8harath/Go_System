# E-file diagnostic: Business Activity Ratio (BAR) Line 11

```
Business Activity Ratio (BAR) Line 11 - Other Business Income: The amount must equal to the total amount on Organizer/ States/ Iowa/ Allocation and Apportionment/ Apportionment Detail, Other Business Income.
```

This happens when Other Business Income amount is not equal to the total amount on Organizer.

## Solution

- Go to **Tax Forms**, **States**, **Iowa** folder.
- Select **Form IA-1120** and go to **Page 5**.
- Go to the **Schedule E - Business Activity Ratio (BAR)** section. The **Line 11 Column A** and **Column B** amounts need to match the amounts in step 6.
- Go to **Organizer**, **States**, **Iowa** folder.
- Select **Allocation and Apportionment** and go to the **Apportionment Detail** section.
- In the **Other Business Income** sub section, the **Total** columns for **Within** and **Everywhere** need to match the amounts in step 3.
- Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
