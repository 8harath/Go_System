```
Message Filer is not registered with DC for this tax type. You must complete New Business Registration (FR-500) on https://mytax.dc.gov/ prior to filing to open an appropriate account
```

The District of Columbia requires business registration through Form FR-500 before filing returns.

## Cause

This reject occurs when you aren't registered with DC for the tax type you're filing. DC requires business registration before you can e-file. Verify your registration status directly with DC before filing.

## Solution

- [Register your business online with the District of Columbia](https://mytax.dc.gov/) using the New Business Registration form (FR-500). You must register online—no other registration method is available.
- After you complete registration, resubmit your return.

note

Thomson Reuters can't determine whether you have registered with the District of Columbia.
