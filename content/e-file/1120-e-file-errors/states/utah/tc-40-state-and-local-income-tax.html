E-file Diagnostic: Utah TC-40, State and Local income tax

Utah TC-40: State and Local income tax taken as an itemized deduction entered on TC-40, line 14 is limited to $10,000
This happens when line 14 on the TC-40 is over $10,000.
Solution
  1. Go to the
    Organizer
    ,
    State
     folder.
  2. Go to the
    Utah
    ,
    Adjustments to Federal Amounts
     folder.
  3. Go to the
    State income tax deducted as itemized deduction (override)
     line and enter
    10,000
    .
  4. Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
