E-file diagnostic: Form GA 4562 Attachment

Form GA 4562 Attachment: The presence of Form GA 4562 was detected. This form must be attached as a PDF. If PDF is attached, mark the checkbox titled "GA Form 4562" to indicate that PDF has been attached.

Solution

To clear this diagnostic, Form GA 4562 must be attached as a PDF file.
  1. Print Georgia Form 4562 to a PDF.
  2. In
    Organizer
    , go to the
    Federal E-file
     folder and select
    Attachments
    .
  3. Select
    PDF Attachments
    , then
    Summary
    .
  4. Select
    Attach PDF
    .
  5. Select
    Add
    .
  6. Select
    Georgia
     as the Attachment Jurisdiction, then
    Next
    .
  7. Select your attachment, then
    Open
    .
  8. Select
    Predefined description
     and select
    Form 4562 Depreciation
  9. Select
    Upload
    , then
    Done
    .
