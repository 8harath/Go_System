Verification of all names, SSNs, and addresses in return required e-file error

The Georgia Department of Revenue requires verification of all names, SSNs, and addresses included in the return. Please verify the data and go to Organizer / States / Georgia / General Information / Verification of Specific Data Requirement for Filing / Check the box.
This e-file error will show if the checkbox wasn't marked for Verification of Specific data.

Solution

  • In Organizer, select
    States
    , then
    Georgia
    .
  • Select
    General Information
    .
  • Mark the
    Verification of Specific Data Requirement For Filing
     checkbox.
  • Perform a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
