Form 1065 federal attachments required by state

Identify the federal attachments required when filing a Form 1065 partnership return with each state or local jurisdiction.
Show all hidden content
The following list identifies the federal attachments required to accompany the state return for each state or locality.
Alabama
  • Complete federal return including all federal K-1s, statements, and attachments
  • A list of all omitted schedules must be provided, if requested
Alaska
  • Form 1065: Pages 1–5
Arizona
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
California
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Colorado
  • No federal attachments required
Connecticut
  • Form 1065, pages 1–5, without Schedule K-1s
  • Taxpayers completing Form CT-1065/CT-1120SI — Connecticut Composite Income Tax Return are now required to attach copies of federal Form 1065 to paper-filed returns only
  • Don’t attach copies of Schedule K-1s
Delaware
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
District of Columbia
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
District of Columbia UBT
  • No federal attachments required
Florida
  • No federal attachments required
Florida LLC
  • No federal attachments required
Georgia
  • Complete federal return including supporting schedules
Hawaii
  • No federal attachments required
Idaho
  • No federal attachments required
Illinois
  • Don’t submit PDF file formats as a federal attachment
  • Only federal Form 1065 forms and schedules are currently accepted
Indiana
  • No federal attachments required
Iowa
  • No federal attachments required
Kentucky
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Kentucky Cities: Fayette County
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Kentucky Cities: Lexington-Fayette Urban County
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Kentucky Cities: Louisville
  • Form 1065, Pages 1–5
Louisiana
  • No federal attachments required
Maine
  • Form 1065, Pages 1–5
Maryland
  • No federal attachments required
  • Maryland Schedules K-1 must be attached when the Pass-Through Entity (PTE) is the recipient of a credit for which the PTE must account (for example, the PTE was a nonresident entity and had taxes paid on its behalf, or was a member of another PTE that was entitled to pass on a tax credit to its members). This includes all Form 510, Schedules B, and any Schedule K-1s showing credits earned by PTE.
Massachusetts
  • No federal attachments required
Michigan
  • Form 1065, Pages 1–5
Minnesota
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Mississippi
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Missouri
  • If you select No on questions 1 and 2 on Form MO-1065, attach a copy of federal Form 1065 and all its schedules, including Schedule K-1
  • If you select Yes on question 2 on Form MO-1065, attach a copy of federal Form 1065 and all its schedules, including Schedule K-1
Missouri Cities: Kansas City
  • Schedule K-1 only
Missouri Cities: St. Louis
  • Schedule K
  • Schedule A
Montana
  • If the partnership is filing a return on paper, assemble the return without using staples, in the following order:
    • All Forms 1099
    • Form PR-1
    • Supporting Montana schedules (including K-1s)
    • Copy of federal return
    • Copy of federal schedules (including K-1s)
    • A copy of the returns from another state if the partnership is a multi-state partnership
Nebraska
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
  • Schedule K-1s
    • If more than 50 federal Schedule K-1s, submit on CD-R media rather than on paper
New Hampshire
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
New Jersey
  • No federal attachments required
New Mexico
  • Form 1065, Pages 1–5
New York
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
New York City
  • No federal attachments required
New York IT-204LL
  • No federal attachments required
North Carolina
  • Don’t attach a copy of federal Form 1065 or copies of K-1s to Form D-403
  • If copies are needed, the state Department of Revenue will request them later
North Dakota
  • Form 1065, Pages 1–5
  • Schedule K-1
Ohio
  • Schedule K-1
Ohio Cities: CCA
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Ohio Cities: Cincinnati
  • Form 1065, Pages 1–5
  • Schedule K-1 (all)
Ohio Cities: RITA
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Oklahoma
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Oregon
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
  • Form 1065-B, including statements and schedules
Pennsylvania
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Pennsylvania Cities: Philadelphia
  • Don’t staple, fold, or submit photocopies of the tax return
  • Don’t submit copies of federal returns
  • File only required schedules
Rhode Island
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
South Carolina
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Tennessee
  • No federal attachments required
Texas
  • No federal attachments required
Utah
  • No federal attachments required
Vermont
  • Form BI-471
  • Form 1065, Pages 1–5
  • All supporting Vermont schedules and statements
Virginia
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
West Virginia
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
Wisconsin
  • Complete federal return including statements and schedules
