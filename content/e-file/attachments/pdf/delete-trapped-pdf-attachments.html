Delete trapped PDF attachments

Clear PDF attachments that appear in the Organizer's summary but don't show when you try to attach them.
Follow these steps to delete trapped attachments:
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    Federal E-file
    Attachments
    PDF Attachments
    Summary
    .
  2. Select
    Attach PDF
    .
  3. Add any document as a PDF to this return.
  4. Close the
    PDF Attachment
     window.
  5. Select
    Attach PDF
     again to re-open the window.
  6. Delete the PDF you attached and close the window.
  7. Perform a full recompute.
The trapped PDF attachments should now be gone.
