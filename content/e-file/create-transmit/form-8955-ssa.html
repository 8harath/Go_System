E-file Form 8955-SSA for separated participants with deferred vested benefits

Follow these steps to e-file Form 8955-SSA, Annual Registration Statement Identifying Separated Participants with Deferred Vested Benefits.
Follow these steps to e-file Form 8955-SSA:
  1. Complete Form 8955-SSA in
    Organizer
    8955-SSA
    Deferred Vested Benefits
    .
  2. Go to
    Organizer
    E-file
    Create/Enable E-file
    .
  3. Select
    Enable Annual Registration Statement E-file
    .
  4. Select
    Create Annual Statement E-file
    .
  5. To review any messages generated by the e-file creation, go to the
    Detail
     tab.
  6. Review diagnostics by selecting
    View
    , then
    Diagnostics
    . Clear any alerts and rejection diagnostics.
  7. To send qualified e-files, go to
    RS Browser
    Returns Processing
    E-file
    Select Returns
    .
  8. Select an account, tax year,
    5500
     for the return type, and
    Other Forms
     for the e-file type.
  9. Select
    Federal ready to send
    , then
    Continue
    .
  10. Select the return, then
    Submit for E-file
    .
