# E-file Form 8955-SSA for separated participants with deferred vested benefits

Follow these steps to e-file Form 8955-SSA, Annual Registration Statement Identifying Separated Participants with Deferred Vested Benefits.

Follow these steps to e-file Form 8955-SSA:

- Complete Form 8955-SSA in 

  **Organizer****8955-SSA****Deferred Vested Benefits**

  .
- Go to 

  **Organizer****E-file****Create/Enable E-file**

  .
- Select **Enable Annual Registration Statement E-file**.
- Select **Create Annual Statement E-file**.
- To review any messages generated by the e-file creation, go to the **Detail** tab.
- Review diagnostics by selecting **View**, then **Diagnostics**. Clear any alerts and rejection diagnostics.
- To send qualified e-files, go to 

  **RS Browser****Returns Processing****E-file****Select Returns**

  .
- Select an account, tax year, **5500** for the return type, and **Other Forms** for the e-file type.
- Select **Federal ready to send**, then **Continue**.
- Select the return, then **Submit for E-file**.
