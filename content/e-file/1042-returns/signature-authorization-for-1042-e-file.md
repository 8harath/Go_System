# Signature authorization for 1042 e-file

These are the signature authorizations for 1042 e-files: Forms 8453-WH and 8879-WH.

## Form 8453-WH

Form 8453-WH, E-file Declaration for Form 1042.

Go to **Organizer**, **Informational Forms**, **Withholding for Foreign Persons**, **Electronic Filing**, and then **Signature Authorization**. If Form 8453's selected, you must upload the form as a binary attachment in the **Attachments**, **Summary** screen. When attaching the form, make sure to select from the pre-defined descriptions.

## Form 8879-WH

Form 8879-WH, E-file Authorization for Form 1042.
