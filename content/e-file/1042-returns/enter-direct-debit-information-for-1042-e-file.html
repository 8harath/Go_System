Enter direct debit information for 1042 e-file

important
The IRS doesn't support direct deposit when e-filing Form 1042.
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    ,
    Information Forms
    ,
    Withholding for Foreign Persons
    ,
    Electronic Filing
    ,
    Additional Information
    , and then
    Bank Information
    .
  2. Select
    Add new (or update existing) account type, routing and account numbers
    , and then enter the bank information on the screen.
