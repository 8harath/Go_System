State electronic filing details

E-filing information
Details
Grace period
5 calendar days. If the fifth day falls on a weekend or holiday, then the next business day will be the final day to retransmit and considered timely.
E-filing mandate
Must e-file if preparing more than 100 returns. Sign and file Form 8454 to opt-out.
Accepts state-only filings
Yes, for nonresident returns only. Attach the federal XML created for this State (Default). Only Federal e-file validation errors have to be cleared. E-file Reject Diagnostics do not have to be cleared
Return types accepted
Resident (Form 540)
Part-year resident (Form 540NR)
Nonresident (Form 540NR)
Amended (Schedule X)
Next year estimate payments (up to 4 with Form 540/540NR)
Extension payment
Estimate Payments with extension
Limited Liability Company (Form 568 original, including next year's LLC tax and next year's LLC fee; Form 568 amended)
Return types not supported
LLC extension
Accepts returns with these conditions
Refund
Zero balance
Balance due
Direct deposit of refunds
Yes, up to 2 accounts for original Form 540/540NR only. No for Form 540/540NR amended, Form 568 original or amended.

Optional
