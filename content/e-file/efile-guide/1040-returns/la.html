E-filing information
Details
Grace period
5 calendar days
E-filing mandate
You must e-file at least 90% of returns if you are preparing more than 100 returns. All Nonresident Professional Athlete returns must be e-filed.
Accepts state-only filings
Yes. Attach the federal XML created for this State (Default). Only Federal e-file validation errors have to be cleared. E-file Reject Diagnostics do not have to be cleared.

Tax year(s) supported for e-filing
Current tax year and two prior tax years
Special considerations
Taxpayers who e-file and are due a federal & state refund and direct deposit the federal refund must also direct deposit the state refund unless the taxpayer lacks a personal checking account. For more information see Handbook for Electronic Filers.
