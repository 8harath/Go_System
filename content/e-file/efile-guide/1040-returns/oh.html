E-filing information
Details
Grace period
7 days
E-filing mandate
Yes, if:
  • You file more than 11 original tax returns (not amended) tax returns during any calendar year;
  • You prepared at least 10 original tax returns during the previous calendar year; and
  • OH Dept of Taxation hasn’t granted you an exclusion (yearly).
Opt-out / Waiver: Form IT Waiver is mailed with paper return.
Accepts state-only filings
Yes. Attach the federal XML created for this State (Default). Only Federal e-file validation errors have to be cleared. E-file Reject Diagnostics do not have to be cleared.
