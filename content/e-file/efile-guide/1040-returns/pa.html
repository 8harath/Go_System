note
Payments of $15,000 or more are required to be paid using EFW, EFT, or by credit card. To pay using EFT or credit card go to mypath.pa.gov.
You may also pay by a credit card by telephone by calling 1-800-272-9829.
EFW and EFT payments can be made from banks located in the U.S.
