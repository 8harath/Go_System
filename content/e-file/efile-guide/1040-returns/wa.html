State electronic filing details

E-filing information
Details
E-filing mandate
WFTC Applications prepared by tax software must be filed electronically
Capital Gains Tax Returns prepared by tax software must be filed electronically
Accepts state-only filings
No. Federal return must be filed electronically and accepted before WA e-file can be processed by WADOR.
Return types accepted
Working Families Tax Credit Application
Capital Gains Tax Return
Conditions for e-filing
Working Families Tax Credit amount must be greater than zero.
Capital Gains - Refund, Zero balance, Balance due
Direct deposit of refunds
Yes for Original application. No for Amended application.
Accepted forms of payment
Balance due is possible only for Amended application. WA DOR will send an invoice to the applicant.
Direct debit (EFW) is available for Capital Gains Tax Return.
Signature requirements
Uses the same PIN used for federal return.
Driver's license requirements
Optional
Supports PDF attachments
Yes
Tax years supported for e-filing
Working Family Tax Credit applications for tax years 2022 - 2024 will be accepted through December 31, 2025. Capital Gains tax returns for 2024 will be accepted through December 31, 2025.
