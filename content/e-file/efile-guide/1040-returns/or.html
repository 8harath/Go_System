Special considerations
  • Portland Arts tax forms only allow one individual that is 18+ years on an e-filed form. If you're filing a joint return or have dependents that are 18 years or older, you'll need to paper file.
  • You can use the Filing Status Optimization (FSO) utility to split a MFJ return in order to e-file the Portland Arts forms.
  • If you e-file the Arts tax forms, you must use electronic funds withdrawal (EFW).
