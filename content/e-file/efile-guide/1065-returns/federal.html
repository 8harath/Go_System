Electronic filing details

E-filing information
Details
Grace period
10 calendar days for returns, 5 calendar days for extensions
E-filing mandate
Partnerships with more than 100 partners and partnerships who file at least 10 returns annually must e-file. These returns include income tax, excise tax, employment tax, and information returns such as Forms W-2 and 1099..
Return types accepted
1065, Partnership
8865, Foreign Partnership, when filed with the parent filer
Short year
Extension (for both 1065 and 8804)
Amended
Superseded
Return types not accepted
8865, Foreign Partnership, when filed separately

Accepts returns with these conditions

No balance due
Balance due
Direct deposit of refunds
No

Electronic funds withdrawal (EFW)
Electronic funds transfer (EFT)

Signature requirements
Signed Form 8453-PE attached to the e-file as a PDF attachment before creating the e-file

PIN and esignatures are allowed.

Yes
Tax year(s) supported for e-filing
Current tax year and two prior tax years
