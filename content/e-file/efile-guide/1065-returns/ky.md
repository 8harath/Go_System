|

**Accepted forms of payment** | Direct debit (EFW)  Check by mail  Electronic funds transfer (EFT) |


|

**Signature requirements** | ERO retains signed 8879(C)-K for 4 years.  PIN is allowed as signature. |


|

**Supports PDF attachments** | Yes |


|

**Tax year(s) supported for e-filing** | Current tax year and two prior tax years |
