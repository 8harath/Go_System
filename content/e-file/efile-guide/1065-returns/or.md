**Accepted forms of payment** | Direct debit (EFW)  Electronic funds transfer (EFT)  Check by mail |


|

**Signature requirements** | PIN is allowed as signature |


|

**Supports PDF attachments** | Yes |


|

**Tax year(s) supported for e-filing** | Current tax year and two prior tax years |


|
