E-filing information
Details
Grace period
24 hours
E-filing mandate
Form 1120ME if a corporation has more than $5,000,000 in assets at the end of the year.
For returns filed in calendar year 2025 and subsequent years, corporations and a preparer for such corporation required to file the federal corporate return electronically for the taxable year must also file the Maine Form 1120ME electronically.
Form 941P-ME for all corporations.

Maine doesn't require a signature document. However, the taxpayer needs to verify the information in the return, and the ERO needs to provide a perjury statement or attestation to the taxpayer prior to the time of submission.
