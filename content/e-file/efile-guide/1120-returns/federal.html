Electronic filing details

E-filing information
Details
Grace period
10 calendar days
E-filing mandate
Corporations who have assets for $10 million or more, and who file at least 10 returns annually. These returns include income tax, excise tax, employment tax, and information returns such as Forms W-2 and 1099.
Return types accepted
1120, C Corporations
1120-L, Life Insurance Companies
1120-PC, Property and Casualty Insurance companies
1120-REIT, Real Estate Investment Trusts
1120-RIC, Regulated Investment Companies
1120S, S Corporations
1120-F, Foreign Corporations
1120-H, Homeowners Associations
1120-POL, Political Organizations

Signature requirements
ERO retains 8879-CORP for C, S, F, H, returns and 8879-TE or 8453-TEfor POL. PIN and esignatures are allowed.
Supports PDF attachments
Yes
Tax year(s) supported for e-filing
