# 1120 federal electronic filing information

## Contact information

If you have specific questions about what you can e-file or about rejections you received, contact the IRS for more information.

**Internal Revenue Service**

**Website**: <https://www.irs.gov/e-file-providers/e-file-for-tax-professionals>

**Phone**: 866-255-0654

## Electronic filing details

|

E-filing information | Details |


| --- | --- |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
|

**Grace period** | 10 calendar days |


|

**E-filing mandate** | Corporations who have assets for $10 million or more, and who file at least 10 returns annually. These returns include income tax, excise tax, employment tax, and information returns such as Forms W-2 and 1099. |


|

**Return types accepted** | 1120, C Corporations  1120-L, Life Insurance Companies  1120-PC, Property and Casualty Insurance companies  1120-REIT, Real Estate Investment Trusts  1120-RIC, Regulated Investment Companies  1120S, S Corporations  1120-F, Foreign Corporations  1120-H, Homeowners Associations  1120-POL, Political Organizations  Consolidated/Combined  Short year  Estimates  Extension  Amended  Superseded |


|

**Return types not accepted** | 1120-C, Cooperative Associations  1120-SF, Settlement Funds |


|

**Accepts returns with these conditions** | Refund/credit  Zero balance  Balance due |


|

**Direct deposit of refunds** | Yes |


|

**Accepted forms of payment** | Electronic funds withdrawal (EFW)  Electronic funds transfer (EFT)  Credit card |


|

**Signature requirements** | ERO retains 8879-CORP for C, S, F, H, returns and 8879-TE or 8453-TEfor POL. PIN and esignatures are allowed. |


|

**Supports PDF attachments** | Yes |


|

**Tax year(s) supported for e-filing** | Current tax year and two prior tax years |


|

**Special considerations** | Refer to[IRS Publication 4163](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p4163.pdf) for detailed e-filing information for business returns. |
