Grace period
10 days
E-filing mandate
  • Firms preparing 10 or more corporate returns in a calendar year
  • Corporations with $5 million of assets,
  • Corporations with 50 or more shareholders at the end of the taxable year
  • Financial institution groups must e-file Business Privilege Tax returns
Accepts state-only filings
No, you must file a federal return with, or prior to filing the AL return.

Tax year(s) supported for e-filing
Current tax year and two prior tax years through 9/1. Then current plus one prior year until 12/31.
Special considerations
For Business Privilege Tax extension payments less than $750, payment can be made by check or money order. For payments $750 and up, electronic payment is mandatory.
Business Privilege Tax returns with amounts due of less than $100 cannot be e-file unless there is a refund requested.
