# 1041 Pennsylvania electronic filing information

## State contact information

If you have specific questions about what you can e-file or about rejections you received, contact the state for more information.

**Pennsylvania Department of Revenue**

[http://www.revenue.pa.gov](http://www.revenue.pa.gov/)

**Email:** [Submit a Question](https://revenue-pa.custhelp.com/app/ask)

**Phone:** 717-787-8327
