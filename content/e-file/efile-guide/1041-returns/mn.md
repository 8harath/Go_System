# 1041 Minnesota electronic filing information

## State contact information

If you have specific questions about what you can e-file or about rejections you received, contact the state for more information.

**Minnesota Department of Revenue - Electronic Information-Filing**

<https://www.revenue.state.mn.us/modernized-e-file-information>

**Email:** [Efile.PrepSupport@state.mn.us](mailto:Efile.PrepSupport@state.mn.us)
