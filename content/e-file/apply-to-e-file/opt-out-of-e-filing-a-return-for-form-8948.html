Opt out of e-filing a return with Form 8948

Use Form 8948 to explain why you're filing a return on paper instead of electronically.
  1. Go to
    Organizer
    E-file
    Additional Information
    E-file Opt Out
    .
  2. Select
    Preparer Explanation
    , then select the reason for not e-filing the return:
    • The taxpayer has selected to file on paper and the return is prepared by the preparer, but will be submitted by mail by the taxpayer.
       A taxpayer statement prints when you select this option.
    • The preparer's a member of a recognized religious group that is opposed to its members using e-file
      .
    • The preparer applied for and received an approved undue hardship waiver.
       Enter the waiver reference number and approval letter date.
    • The return was rejected and the reject condition couldn't be resolved.
       Enter the reject code and the number of attempts to solve the rejection.
    • The tax product doesn't support the e-filing of one or more forms or schedules
      .
  3. If none of these reasons apply, select
    Other
     and describe the circumstances.
