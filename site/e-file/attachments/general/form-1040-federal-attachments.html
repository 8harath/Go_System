Form 1040 federal attachments required by state

Identify the federal attachments required when filing a Form 1040 return with each state or local jurisdiction.

Show all hidden content

The following list identifies the federal attachments required to accompany the state return for each state or locality.

Alabama

  • Form 2106
  • Form 3903
  • Form 4684
  • Form 4797
  • Form 6252
  • Form 8283
  • Schedule C
  • Schedule F

Alaska

  • N/A

Arizona

  • If you amend your federal return for any year, attach a complete copy of the federal notice to your Form 140X
  • Schedule A if itemizing on the AZ return

California

  • Complete federal return
  • If you attach any federal forms or schedules other than Schedule A or Schedule B to Form 1040, attach a copy of your 1040 return and all supporting federal forms and schedules to Form 540

Colorado

  • N/A

Connecticut

  • N/A

Delaware

  • Form 1040/1040SR, Pages 1 & 2
  • All federal schedules that are required to be filed with the federal return.
  • Form 2441
  • Form EIC
  • Proforma federal return if entered into a civil union in the current year
  • If a taxpayer elects to file a nonresident return, the attachment is the same as the nonresident

note

Proforma federal return is required. Form 5884 (Work Opportunity Credit) is not included because the DE program doesn’t know what amount of other subtractions are related to the federal credit. The DE program must be enhanced before we can attach the federal form.

  • Form 1116
  • Form 1310
  • Form 4868
  • Form 8839, Parts I, II, III (if claiming the adoption credit K-47 for the 1st year) — resident only
