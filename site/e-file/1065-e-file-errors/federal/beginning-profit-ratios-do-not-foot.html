Severe Diagnostic: Beginning profit ratios do not foot

Beginning profit ratios do not foot to 1.0: Check beginning profit ratios entered in Organizer.

Make sure the beginning ratios add to 1.0. Zeros and NONE will also trigger this error.

Solution

note

This is a severe diagnostic, it will not stop you from e-filing. Even if this is a first year filer beginning ratios are expected because the program is looking for the ratios at the beginning of the year.

  • Go to

OrganizerPartner InformationPartner by Partner DataColumnar Partner Entry

. - Go to the Ratios\Units tab. - Make sure the beginning ratios for the partners add up to 1.0.
