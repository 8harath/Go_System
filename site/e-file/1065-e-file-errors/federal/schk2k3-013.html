SCHK2K3-013 e-file error

If [SchedulesK2K3Section743bBasisAdjustmentsStatement] is present in the return, then it must be referenced from one and only one of the following: Schedule K-2 (Form 1065) or Schedule K-3 (Form 1065) or Schedule K-2 (Form 8865) or Schedule K-3 (Form 8865).

Solution

  • Go to Organizer, then Foreign Information.
  • Select Ptr Distributive Share-International, then Other Tax Information-III.
  • Review each asset entered in Sec 743(b) Basis Adj for Pos Inc Adj and/or Sec 743(b) Basis Adj for Neg Inc Adj. Only assets from outside of the United States should be entered.

  • For any assets where the source of income is from the United States:

  • Remove the amounts from here.

  • Go to Other Tax Information, also in the Other Tax Information-III folder.
  • Enter the amounts in the U.S. source fields for either section A- Section 743(b) Positive Income Adjustment or B- Section 743(b) Negative Income Adjustment
  • This transfers to Schedule K-2, Page 8, Part III, Section 5.
  • Perform a full recompute and recreate the Federal e-file.
