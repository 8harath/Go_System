NCBUS-1200 e-file error

NCBUS-1200 - Associated K-1's Received - [NC K-1 Supplemental Schedule - C: D401S/D403/D407] Part A Line 16 must equal the sum of Lines 1 through 15 for Column A (Amount from All Sources) and Column B (Amount of Column A from N.C. Sources).

This happens when you are missing information required by the state. Sch NC K-1 Supp, Page 1 Part A Line 16 must equal the sum of Lines 1 through 15 for Column A (Amount from All Sources) and Column B (Amount of Column A from N.C. Sources).

Solution 1

  • Go to Organizer, States, and the North Carolina folder.
  • Select the Additional K-1 Information, Other Partnership folder.
  • Select Partnership Name or create a [CREATEPARTNERSHIP].
  • The K-1 received input for the partnership is missing input amounts for the Total Additions line under the Additions section, and the Total Subtractions line under the Subtraction section. The application can't automatically calculate these fields. This must be entered manually to match the K-1 received exactly.
  • Do a full recompute, and recreate the e-file.

Solution 2

  • Go to Tax forms, States, and the North Carolina folder.
  • Go to Sch NC K-1 folder.
  • Select the Partner Name.
  • Go to Sch NC K-1 Supp, Page 1 tab.
  • Go to Part A Line 16 must equal the sum of Lines 1 through 15 for Column A (Amount from All Sources) and Column B (Amount of Column A from N.C. Sources).
  • Do a full recompute, and recreate the e-file.

Solution 3

  • Go to Organizer, States, and the North Carolina folder.
  • Select the State Adjustments folder.
  • Go to the Nonapportionable Net Distributive Partnership Income section.
  • Enter an explanation in the Explanation of why income is listed is nonapportionable income; rather than apportionable income area.
  • Do a full recompute, and recreate the e-file.

Solution 4

  • Go to Tax Forms, States, North Carolina folder.
  • Select Form D-403. In D-403, Page 2, Part 3 See Detail hyperlink, enter NONE for all blank columns.
  • Do a full recompute, and recreate the e-file.
