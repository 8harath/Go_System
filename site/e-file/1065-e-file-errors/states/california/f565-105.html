📍 California (CA) Form e-file F565-105

F565-105 e-file error

If Form 565 Question Q or Form 568 Question S is checked "Yes", then Schedule EO Part I must have data and ownership must be less than 100%.

If you don't answer Yes for Sch EO Part II Question Z or S, Schedule EO Part II won't print.

Solution 1

  • Go to Organizer, States, California, Other State Forms, then Schedule EO.
  • For Pass-Through Entity Ownership, enter Sch EO information for both Part I and Part II.
  • Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.

Solution 2

  • Go to Organizer, States, California, Additional Information, then Questions.
  • Select No for Line Q/S.
  • Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.

Solution 3

  • Go to Organizer, States, California, Additional Information, then Questions.
  • Select Yes for Line BB/Z, Does this partnership/LLC own an interest in a business entity disregarded for tax purposes?
  • Do a full recompute, re-create the e-file, and resubmit the return.
