📍 California (CA) Form e-file F565

F565/568-112 e-file error

CA Rule F565/568-112: If Form 565 Question BB or Form 568 Question Z is checked "Yes", then Schedule EO Part II must have data. Navigation: Organizer>States>California>Other State Forms>Schedule EO.

This happens when California Form 565, Side 3 Question BB or Form 568, Side 2 Line Z is checked "Yes" and Schedule EO Part II is empty.

Solution 1

If you didn’t mean to answer Form 565 Question BB or Form 568 Question Z with Yes, you can change your answer.

  • In Organizer, select States, then California, then Additional Information.
  • SelectQuestions, then BB/Z Does this partnership/LLC own interest in a business entity disregarded for tax purposes?
  • Change you answer to No.
  • Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file

Solution 2

If you need to add information to Schedule EO Part II,

  • In Tax Forms, select States, then California.
  • Select 568, then 568, then 568, Side 2, line Z.
  • IfDoes this LLC own an interest in a business entity disregarded for tax purposes? is Yes, complete Schedule EO, Part II.
  • Complete Schedule EO in Organizer.
  • Select

StatesCaliforniaOther State FormsSchedule EO

. - Part I - Partial Ownership of Less than 100% and Part II – Full Ownership of 100% must have at least one entry with all the information filled out. - Do a full-recompute and recreate the e-file.
