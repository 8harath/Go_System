E-file diagnostic: NY IT-204 e-file: According to New York

NY IT-204 E-File: According to New York reject code R1143. If IT-204 Page 7, Line 117c, "Is this partnership a partner in another partnership or LLC" is "Yes". Then Name of entity and EIN MUST be entered. If the partnership is a Foriegn then Enter NY6666666 as an EIN or if Applied For was used then enter NY7777777 as an EIN.

This happens when you haven’t entered the Name of Entity and EIN if the Partnership in Another Partnership or LLC Information is Yes.

Solution

  • Go to the Organizer, States, New York folder.
  • Select State Information, then go to Business Address tab.
  • In Section 9 - Other Information section.
  • Go to the Partnership in Another Partnership or LLC Information sub section.
  • If you have selected YES for Is this partnership a partner in another partnership or LLC.
  • Enter the Names and EINs in Federal Schedule B, Ownership of Other Partnerships section and attach additional sheets if necessary.
  • Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.

note

As per the New York e-file each entry must have an EIN, also New York doesn’t allow Foreign or non-EIN entries. If the Partnership has a Foreign EIN, enter NY6666666 or if Applied For enter NY7777777. If Foreign U.S., or "999999999" or Applied For was entered in the Federal Schedule B, it will automatically be converted to the NY specifications.
