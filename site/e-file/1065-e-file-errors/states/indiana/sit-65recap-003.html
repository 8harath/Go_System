📍 Indiana (IN) Form e-file 10651065-US

SIT-65RECAP-003 e-file error

*SIT-65RECAP-003: Indiana Federal Recap Taxes and License total does not match the sum of Taxes and License details. Please check organizer \ Ordinary income and Deductions \ Deduction summary \ Deductions Detail. Make sure there is an amount on the first line item. Maximum detail items allowed for Taxes and License entries is limited to 20.

This happens when Federal Form 1065-US Partnership Return of Income Page 1 Line 14 Taxes and Licenses total doesn’t match Taxes and License Detail.

Solution

  • In Tax Forms, select

Federal1065 - US Partnership Return of IncomePage 1Line 14Taxes and Licenses

. - On Line 14, go to the

Workpaper Taxes & LicensesGeneral trade or business lineWorkpaperDescription

. - This will take you to

OrganizerOrdinary Income and DeductionsDeductions SummaryDeductions SummaryTaxes and Licenses

, then enter an amount. - Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
