2138 e-file error

2138: Total Other Subtractions from Partnership Income on Schedule B, Line B5 is incorrect. The sum of lines A through G does not match the amount in Line B5-H.

This happens when Schedule B line B5 doesn’t match Schedule B line H.

Solution

  • Tax Forms, then States.
  • Select Arizona, then 165.
  • Select the 165, Page 2 tab.
  • Go to the Part 1 – Adjustment of Partnership income From Federal to Arizona Basis area.
  • Remember the amount on line B5.
  • Then go to 165, Page 6 .
  • Go toSchedule B. The amount on line H needs to match the amount on line B5.
  • To adjust the amount, select Organizer, then States .
  • Select Arizona, then Schedule A.
  • Go to the B5 area.
  • On the line Negative Partnership income adjustment, enter the amount that was on line H so that line B5 will have the same amount.
  • Run a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
