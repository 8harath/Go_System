2137 e-file error

2137: The calculation for Total Other Additions to Partnership Income on Schedule A, Line A4 is incorrect.

This happens when an amount exists on Form 165, page 1, line A4, but no matching amount is present on Form 165, page 6, line A4, D.

Solution

  • In Tax forms, selectStates, thenArizona
  • Select 165, and then drill-down on line 1 A4.
  • Drill down on the first amount in the worksheet
  • Make a note of the detail for Other Additions and the total.
  • In Organizer, selectStates, thenArizona.
  • Select Schedule A and go to the section: A4.
  • Enter detail the same as what you noted previously to arrive at the total for Other Additions.
