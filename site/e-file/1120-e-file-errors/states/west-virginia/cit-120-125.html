CIT-120-125 e-file error

WV E-file: CIT-120-125 Sum of the All Adjusted Sales for each entity must equal to combined everywhere Adjusted Sales amount Navigation:Tax form >West Virginia > Combined Report > Apportionment- member informtion. sum of total adjusted sales col 2 everywhere for each member should add up to amount shown on CIT-120APT Col 2 line 6.

This happens when in West Virginia the sum of each entity's Everywhere adjusted sales (found in Column 2 for each member) is not equal to the total Everywhere adjusted sales reported on Form CIT-120APT, Column 2, Line 6.

Solution 1

  • Go to

Tax FormsStatesWest Virginia

. - Select Form CIT-120APT, then CIT-120APT, Page 2 on the “within” and “everywhere” apportionment amounts to look for amounts in the eliminations. - If you have any amounts in Elim, remove them and adjust the amounts entered in the other member returns. - Do a full recompute. - Reconsolidate the Top Consolidation locator by Step 1 - What to Consolidate , Step 4 - Review Preconsol Checklist and Step 5 – Consolidate . - Recreate the e-file.

Solution 2

  • Make sure there are no elimination amounts for property, payroll, and sales.
  • Go to

Tax FormsStatesWest Virginia

. - Select Combined Report, Apportionment - Member Information, then Member Apportionment. Subsidiary, Apportionment, Sub Detail are populated for every member. Enter NONE for any entities without apportionment. - Do a full re-compute and recreate the e-file.

Solution 3

  • Select Organizer, then State Combined Returns.
  • Select State Consolidation Steps, 6 - Press Delete previously transferred state data, then West Virginia.
  • Select Organizer, then Consolidated Returns.
  • Select Step 1 - What to Consolidate. In the Consolidated Return List section, select Subsidiary Listing complete.
  • Select Organizer, Consolidated Returns, Step 4 - Review Preconsol Checklist, Out of balance and compute pending, then Compute all Subsidiaries now.
  • Select Organizer, Consolidated Returns, then Step 5 – Consolidate. In the Consolidation section, select Consolidate.
  • Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
