E-file diagnostic: Schedule D/J: South Carolina requires at least 1 officer with name 

Schedule D/J: South Carolina requires at least one officer with name, title, and full address be included in the return. For combined returns, you will need to enter officer information for each member

This requirement appears to occur when a business or organization is filing a tax return in South Carolina, specifically a Schedule D/J return, which is likely related to the state's corporate or business tax filing requirements.

Solution

In these situations, the business needs to provide the name, title, and full address of at least 1 officer for each member of the combined return.

  • Go to Organizer, Income and Deductions, Deductions folder.
  • Select Officers Compensation, Individual Officers, then Select/Add Name.
  • Option to Apply Officer Information needs to be on.
  • Select Federal and Selected States or States Only.
  • Select Select States.
  • Mark the checkbox for South Carolina.
  • Enter the address, city, postal code, and country.
  • Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
