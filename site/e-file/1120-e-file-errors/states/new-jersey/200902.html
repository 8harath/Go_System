📍 New Jersey (NJ) Form e-file 200902

200902 e-file error

New Jersey counts tax periods by month. This reject happens when the tax period end date is missing, invalid, or exceeds 12 months. Fiscal year filers and 52/53 week filers often get this error when dates don't align with New Jersey's requirements.

Solution

Complete these steps to resolve the reject:

  • Go to

OrganizerGeneral InformationBasic Return Information

. - Find the Dates section. - Review the Fiscal year beginning and Fiscal year ending dates. The period can't exceed 12 months.

important

New Jersey doesn't have a 52/53 week filer option. For federal 52/53 week returns, override the dates to match the 12-month return dates used for the federal return. - Go to

OrganizerStatesCommon StateGeneral InformationState Dates

. - Locate the Fiscal Year Date Overrides section. - Enter the deemed 12-month return dates used for the federal return. - If the return still gets rejected, contact the New Jersey Division of Taxation to double check the correct dates.
