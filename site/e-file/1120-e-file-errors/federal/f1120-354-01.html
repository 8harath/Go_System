F1120-354-01 e-file error

Form 1120: Rule Number: F1120-354-01 Form 1120, Line 8 must equal Schedule D (Form 1120) Part III Line 18.

This happens when the amount reported on Line 8 of Form 1120 (U.S. Corporation Income Tax Return) does match the amount calculated and reported on Line 18 of Schedule D (Form 1120), which is "Capital Gains and Losses."

Solution

  • Go to Tax Forms, Federal, Sch D/8949-Capital Gains and Losses folder.

  • Remove negative numbers

  • Lines 16,17, and 18 won't populate with negative amounts since the lines refer to gains.

  • Line 18 won't populate if Lines 16 and 17 aren't populated, and won't equal 1120 page 1, line 8.

note

Either the negative amounts will need to be removed, or the return will have to be filed on paper. - Go to Organizer, Income and Deductions, Income folder. - Go to the Line 31-620 Capital gain net income line if no detail is entered on Schedule D. - Clear the Carry Capital Loss to Page 1 checkbox. - Do a full recompute and recreate the e-file.
