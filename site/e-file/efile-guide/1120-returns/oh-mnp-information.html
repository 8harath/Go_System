📍 Ohio (OH) Form e-file 1120

1120 Ohio Municipal Net Profit electronic filing information

State contact information

If you have specific questions about what you can e-file or about rejections you received, contact the municipality for more information. Municipal Net Profit tax information is available on the Ohio Department of Taxation's website.
