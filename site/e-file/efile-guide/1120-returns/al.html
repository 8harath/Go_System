State electronic filing details

|

E-filing information | Details |

| --- | --- |

Grace period | 10 days |

|

E-filing mandate | * Firms preparing 10 or more corporate returns in a calendar year * Corporations with $5 million of assets, * Corporations with 50 or more shareholders at the end of the taxable year * Financial institution groups must e-file Business Privilege Tax returns |

|

Accepts state-only filings | No, you must file a federal return with, or prior to filing the AL return. |

|

Return types accepted | C Corporation S Corporation Consolidated/Combined Short year Composite (PTE-C) Privilege Tax (CPT/PPT) Financial Institution Excise (ET-1) Pass-through Entity Return (EPT) Estimates Extension |

|

Return types not accepted | Amended Withholding |

|

Accepts returns with these conditions | Refund Zero balance Balance due |

|

Direct deposit of refunds | Yes |

|

Accepted forms of payment | Direct debit (EFW) Electronic funds transfer (EFT) Credit card My Alabama Taxes (AL online portal) |

|

Signature requirements | ERO retains signed AL8453-C, AL8453-B, AL8453-PTE, and AL8453-FIE |

|

Supports PDF attachments | No |

|

Tax year(s) supported for e-filing | Current tax year and two prior tax years through 9/1. Then current plus one prior year until 12/31. |

|

Special considerations | For Business Privilege Tax extension payments less than $750, payment can be made by check or money order. For payments $750 and up, electronic payment is mandatory. Business Privilege Tax returns with amounts due of less than $100 cannot be e-file unless there is a refund requested. Accepts federal extension |
