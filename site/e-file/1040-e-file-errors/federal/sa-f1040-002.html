E-file diagnostic: SA-F1040-002

Learn how to attach Form 8283 when Schedule A, Line 12 noncash contributions exceed $500 to resolve e-file errors.

Schedule A: If Schedule A, Line 12 'Other than by Cash or Check' amount is greater than $500, then Form 8283 must be attached to Line 12. (Rule Number: SA-F1040-002)

Cause

This diagnostic occurs when there is an amount greater than $500 on Schedule A, line 12, and Form 8283 is not completed.

Solution 1: Schedule A, line 12

  • Go to

OrganizerItemized Deductions

. - Go to

ContributionsContributions – CY and Carryovers

. - Go to the CURRENT-YEAR NONCASH CONTRIBUTIONS WHICH TOTAL LESS THAN OR EQUAL TO $500 section. - In this section, remove the information because it totals over $500. - Go to the Section A Donated Property or Section B Donated Property folder and add the information. - Do a full recompute, then recreate the e-file.

Solution 2: From a K-1

  • Go to

OrganizerIncome

. - Go to

Schedule K-1(name of K-1)Schedule K-1 Information

. - Go to the Deductions tab. - Select Noncash contributions $500 - $5000 or Noncash contributions over $5000 and enter the information. - Do a full recompute, then recreate the e-file.
