F1040NR-027-01 e-file error

Error

The sum of Form 1040NR, [ WagesSalariesAndTipsAmt and TotalOtherIncomeAmt and Schedule OI, TreatyTaxExemptUSIncomeAmt ] plus (+) the sum of Schedule NEC, [ DividendEquivalentPaymentAmt in IncomeTaxRt30UndTrtyRuleGrp and IncomeTaxRtOthUndTrtyRuleGrp and IncomeTaxRtOth2UndTrtyRuleGrp ] plus (+) [ OtherIncomeAmt in IncomeTaxRt10UndTrtyRuleGrp and IncomeTaxRt15UndTrtyRuleGrp and IncomeTaxRt30UndTrtyRuleGrp and IncomeTaxRtOthUndTrtyRuleGrp and IncomeTaxRtOth2UndTrtyRuleGrp ] plus (+) the sum of all Schedule C (Form 1040), TotalGrossReceiptsAmt plus (+) Schedule C-EZ (Form 1040), TotalGrossReceiptsAmt must be greater than or equal to (the sum of all Forms 1042-S, GrossIncomeAmt with [(1) IncomeTypeCd having the value "17" or "18" or "19" or "20" or "23" or "25" or "26" or "27" or "32" or "35" or "38" or "40" or "41" or "42" or "43" or "44" and "54" and (2) ExemptionCd in Chapter3Grp not having the value "02" or "04"] plus (+) the sum of all Forms W-2, WagesAmt ). (rule 1040NR-027-01)

Cause

This return meets these conditions:

  • Has a Form 1040NR with a Form 1042-S that has income.
  • Uses income codes 17, 18, 19, 20, 23, 25, 26, 27, 32, 35, 38, 40, 41, 42, 43, 44, or 54.
  • The income isn't reported on the 1040NR, Page 4, on the Dividend Equivalent line or on Schedule C.

Solution

File the return on paper.
